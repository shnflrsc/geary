The Office of the Student Financial Assistance and Scholarships (OSFAS) manages government grants, private foundation scholarships, and university tuition discounts at BulSU.

## Government grants

Most national student subsidies at BulSU run through UniFAST and CHED. OSFAS announces openings and validation windows on short notice on its Facebook page.

### Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES)

TES runs through published grantee batch lists and campus validations rather than open walk-in calls, prioritizing students under the updated Listahanan. If your name appears on an OSFAS TES batch list, meet two strict deadlines:

- **Landbank Piso Plus account:** Open a Landbank Piso Plus account before the cutoff announced by OSFAS so grants can be disbursed.
- **Semester validation:** Submit your ID, current Certificate of Registration (COR), PWD ID if applicable, and screenshots of your virtual or physical Landbank card within the 2-to-3-day validation window.

### Tulong Dunong Program (TDP)

TDP calls open separately for the Main Campus and External Campuses, often with **24-hour online submission windows** and strict slot limits. To qualify, you must meet all three rules:

- Officially enrolled in your first undergraduate degree at BulSU. Second-coursers are not eligible.
- Not an existing TES or TDP grantee.
- Not a CHED Merit Scholar or recipient of any other government-funded scholarship.

Prepare these documents ahead of time so you can upload within the 24-hour window:

- Signed Student Application Form (Annex 2), with a 2x2 photo when required by the call
- Signed Certified True Copy of your current COR
- Signed Barangay Certificate of Indigency
- School ID or valid government ID, plus your latest Certificate of Grades (COG) if the call asks for it
- All pages merged into **one single PDF file** following the exact upload instructions in the OSFAS post

### Landbank validation rules for grantees

When OSFAS announces Landbank validation for TES or TDP batches, follow these format rules closely or your submission will be rejected:

- Printed front-and-back copy of your student ID or valid ID with **three original wet signatures** and your active contact number. Electronic signatures are not accepted.
- Clear screenshot of your virtual or physical Landbank Visa Debit card showing all card numbers.
- Screenshot of your Landbank banking app clearly showing your account number.

Check the latest OSFAS post and office instructions before submitting. Recent validation rounds have shifted between Google Form uploads and hard-copy drop-offs.

## GAD Modified Financial Assistance (GAD MFAP)

BulSU's Gender and Development Center runs a separate financial assistance program for qualified students from marginalized groups under the Magna Carta of Women. Eligible groups include indigenous peoples, solo parents, differently-abled students, and abused, abandoned, or orphaned students.

Applications are free and take about **6 to 7 working days** to process through your college or campus Associate Dean and GAD Coordinator.

### Initial application documents

- Application Form and Interview Form, available from your college or campus Associate Dean or GAD Coordinator
- Valid ID photocopy with three specimen signatures
- Photocopy of your current COR and latest COG
- Recent original Meralco bill and recent original water bill
- PWD ID or Barangay or MSWDO Certificate of Indigency, if applicable

Renewals only need the Renewal Form, Interview Form, signed ID photocopy, COR, and COG.

After submitting your documents to your Associate Dean, complete a face-to-face interview with your GAD Coordinator and wait for your qualification notice through your college.

## Institutional tuition discounts and other national grants

Beyond OSFAS partner calls, BulSU recognizes several baseline university discounts and external government grants. Verify current stipend rates directly with OSFAS or the sponsoring agency before applying, since allowances update periodically.

- **High school valedictorians and salutatorians:** Valedictorians receive a 100% first-semester tuition discount and salutatorians receive 50%. You must have graduated from a recognized high school with at least 100 graduates certified by your principal.
- **Academic scholars:** Students with a GPA of **1.50 or better** receive a 100% tuition discount, and students with **1.75 or better** receive 50%. You must have no grade lower than 2.00 across at least 15 academic units over the preceding two semesters.
- **Student Government officers:** Sitting Student Government Presidents qualify for a full tuition grant, and Vice Presidents qualify for a partial grant.
- **BulSU employee dependents:** 100% tuition discount if you have no failing grade in any enrolled subject. Submit your latest COG, your parent's employee ID, and your birth certificate.
- **Cultural talents and varsity athletes:** 50% to 100% tuition discount, recommended by advisers, coaches, and Sports Development based on active group service or SCUAA and UCLAA competition service.
- **Barangay Scholarship:** 100% tuition discount for children of elective barangay officials, excluding tanods.
- **External national and local grants:** BulSU also recognizes DOST, CHED Merit and Grant-in-Aid, ESGP-PA for 4Ps beneficiaries, GSIS, OWWA, PVAO, AFP, and LGU or congressional assistance.

## How OSFAS screens university-administered applicants

University-administered grants and general private benefactors require a baseline GPA of at least 2.00, at least 15 academic units, and no grade of 3.00 unless the donor sets custom terms. OSFAS typically collects your admission files, COG or Form 138, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, electric bill, and Certificate of Good Moral Character.

Applicants are scored on a **100-point rubric**:

| Component | Weight | What OSFAS evaluates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essay test | 20 points | Written reflection on what the scholarship means to you |
| Personality | 20 points | Poise and confidence during the objective test and interview |
| Values | 20 points | Humility, sincerity, truthfulness, and nationalism |
| Reasoning | 20 points | How you plan to use the financial support |
| Economic condition | 20 points | Assessed from utility bills, family size, housing, and parent work |

Once approved and your donor agreement is signed, have OSFAS stamp your scholarship details on your COR before heading to the Accounting Office.

## Keeping or losing your scholarship

Visit OSFAS for a **renewal form** every semester as soon as your grades are released. Failing to meet grade rules can suspend or terminate your grant:

- **Government and congressional grants:** Zero failing grades of 5.00.
- **ESGP-PA:** Failing more than two subjects despite academic intervention terminates the grant.
- **Cultural groups:** One failing mark triggers a warning that you must clear the next semester. Three failing marks end the scholarship.
- **Varsity athletes:** Full scholars cannot fail more than 10% of their enrolled units.
