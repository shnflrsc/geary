BulSU enforces student discipline progressively, starting from verbal counsel and constructive Transformative Experience hours up to suspension, dismissal, or permanent expulsion. Alongside the Student Handbook, the university circulates *Enhanced Guidelines on Student Conduct, Discipline, and Grievance Intervention* through the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) and campus Student Welfare offices. Confirm current sanction updates with your campus OSAS office if you are involved in a case.

## Categories of offenses and sanctions

Penalties escalate with repeat offenses. Committing 3 different violations triggers a 2nd-offense penalty based on your most serious infraction; 4 different violations trigger a 3rd-offense penalty.

### Conduct offenses

| Tier | Common examples | Typical progressive sanctions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Littering, unauthorized posters or flyers, vandalism, class disruption, unauthorized selling or solicitation | 1st: verbal reprimand. 2nd: written reprimand plus 4 hours Transformative Experience. 3rd: 10 hours plus Guidance Intervention, with restitution or cleaning. 4th: 2-week suspension. |
| Less Grave | Smoking, gambling, or alcohol on campus; malicious accusations; fraud or impersonation; disrespecting officials; entering or damaging closed premises | 1st to 2nd: written reprimand plus 4 to 8 hours Transformative Experience and parent dialogue with the Dean. 3rd: 10 to 32 hours plus Guidance Intervention and restitution. 4th: 2-week suspension. |
| Grave | Theft, lewd conduct on campus, physical, verbal, or sexual abuse, cyberbullying, hazing, threats, illegal drugs, carrying weapons | Written reprimand or 1-week to 1-semester suspension, escalating to dismissal or immediate expulsion. Drugs, weapons, or serious injury also carry police turnover. |

### Academic dishonesty

| Violation | 1st offense | 2nd offense | 3rd offense |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheating on exams | Zero score | Failing grade plus 16 hours Transformative Experience | 1-month suspension plus failing grade |
| Plagiarism in theses or papers | Failed output | Failed subject plus 2-week suspension | 1-semester suspension |
| Falsifying or forging academic records or official documents | 1-semester suspension | 1-year suspension | Dismissal |

Transformative Experience hours are scheduled during your vacant periods so they do not interrupt regular classes. The university may also withhold academic records or a degree while a disciplinary case is still pending. Student leaders and Student Government officers face Board of Student Discipline proceedings for official misconduct, such as altering or falsifying project liquidation documents.

### Check plagiarism and AI similarity before submitting

If you are preparing a thesis or major academic output and want guidance on plagiarism or AI checking, visit the **4th or 5th floor of the BulSU e-Library** to inquire with the assigned library staff. For full plagiarism-check steps, read the [BulSU Library Guide](/articles/bulsu-library-guide) article.

## Formal hearing and appeal process

Any person may file a sworn written complaint with witness statements and supporting evidence before the college or campus Student Discipline Committee. The committee is chaired by the College Secretary or campus equivalent, alongside faculty, Student Governor, Parent-Teacher Association (PTA), arbiter, and secretary. Campus press cases go through Student Publications instead.

1. Submit a sworn written answer within **5 working days**. If the committee finds a *prima facie* case, it sends you the complaint and evidence, and you submit your written answer with supporting documents.
2. Attend the closed, non-litigious hearing within **5 working days** of your answer. The committee holds the hearing to clarify facts. You may bring legal counsel at your own expense and submit questions through the committee. If you fail to appear after proper notice, the hearing proceeds without you.
3. Wait for the committee recommendation and Board decision. The committee sends its majority recommendation to the Board of Student Discipline within **5 working days**, and the Board issues its decision within **5 working days**.
4. File a Motion for Reconsideration within **10 working days** of receiving the decision. Either party may file this motion with the Board.
5. Appeal a dismissal or expulsion within **10 working days** if your motion is denied. Direct this final appeal to the University President or Board of Regents. Dismissal means exclusion for at least 1 year, signed by the President; expulsion is permanent severance recorded on your Transcript of Records (TOR).

### Summary proceedings

The university skips formal hearings when a respondent admits guilt in writing, is caught in the act by university authorities, faces overwhelming evidence, or is a habitual offender punished 3 or more times before.

## Expunge a suspension record

Suspensions lasting at least 1 semester are recorded in your permanent file. After **3 years** from the date of the finding, you may petition the University President or their designee to expunge the record based on the severity of the violation, your overall standing, and proof of good conduct since. Once approved, the Registrar removes the suspension notation from your file.

If you need to report unfair treatment, grade issues, or harassment directed at you, read [How to File a Student Complaint](/articles/how-to-file-a-student-complaint).
