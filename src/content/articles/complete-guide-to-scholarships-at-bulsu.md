The Office of the Student Financial Assistance and Scholarships (OSFAS) manages government grants, private foundation scholarships, and university tuition discounts at BulSU.

## OSFAS location and contacts

OSFAS is located in the **Ground Floor of Natividad Hall**.

- Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Email: **scholarship@bulsu.edu.ph**
- Phone: (044) 919 7800 local 1086
- Facebook: [BulSU OSFAS Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUStudentFinancialAssistanceandScholarships)

## Government grants

Most national student subsidies at BulSU run through UniFAST and CHED. OSFAS announces openings and validation windows on short notice on its Facebook page.

### Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES)

TES runs through published grantee batch lists and campus validations (not open walk-in calls), prioritizing students under the updated Listahanan. If your name appears on an OSFAS TES batch list, meet 2 strict deadlines:

- Open a Landbank Piso Plus account before the cutoff announced by OSFAS so grants can be disbursed.
- Submit your ID, current Certificate of Registration (COR), PWD ID if applicable, and screenshots of your virtual or physical Landbank card within the **2-to-3-day validation window**.

### Tulong Dunong Program (TDP)

TDP calls open separately for the Main Campus and External Campuses, often with **24-hour online submission windows** and strict slot limits. To qualify, you must meet all 3 rules:

- Hold official enrollment in your 1st undergraduate degree at BulSU (second-coursers are not eligible)
- Have no existing TES or TDP grant
- Hold no CHED Merit Scholarship or other government-funded scholarship

Prepare these required documents ahead of time so you can upload within the 24-hour window:

- Signed Student Application Form (Annex 2)
- Signed Certified True Copy of your current COR
- Signed Barangay Certificate of Indigency
- School ID or valid government ID

If the specific OSFAS call asks for them, also prepare a 2x2 photo for Annex 2 and your latest Certificate of Grades (COG). Merge all pages into **1 single PDF file** following the exact upload instructions in the OSFAS post.

### Landbank validation requirements for grantees

When OSFAS announces Landbank validation for TES or TDP batches, follow these format rules closely or your submission will be rejected:

- Printed front-and-back copy of your student ID or valid ID with **3 original wet signatures** and your active contact number (electronic signatures are not accepted)
- Clear screenshot of your virtual or physical Landbank Visa Debit card showing all card numbers
- Screenshot of your Landbank banking app clearly showing your account number

Check the latest OSFAS post and office instructions before submitting. Recent validation rounds have shifted between Google Form uploads and hard-copy drop-offs.

## Gender and Development Modified Financial Assistance (GAD MFAP)

BulSU's Gender and Development Center runs a separate financial assistance program for qualified students from marginalized groups under the Magna Carta of Women. Eligible groups include indigenous peoples, solo parents, differently-abled students, and abused, abandoned, or orphaned students.

Applications are free and take about **6 to 7 working days** to process through your college or campus Associate Dean and GAD Coordinator.

### Initial application documents

Prepare these mandatory documents:

- Application Form and Interview Form, available from your college or campus Associate Dean or GAD Coordinator
- Valid ID photocopy with 3 specimen signatures
- Photocopy of your current COR and latest COG
- Recent original Meralco bill and recent original water bill

If applicable, also bring your PWD ID or a Barangay or MSWDO Certificate of Indigency. Renewals only need the Renewal Form, Interview Form, signed ID photocopy, COR, and COG.

After submitting your documents to your Associate Dean, complete a face-to-face interview with your GAD Coordinator and wait for your qualification notice through your college.

## Private and foundation scholarships through OSFAS

Private partners open scholarship slots through OSFAS throughout the academic year. Like TDP, almost every partner enforces **24-to-48-hour online submission windows**, requires **all documents merged into 1 single PDF**, rejects late or incomplete entries, and requires an active email address and phone number.

Recent active partners posted by OSFAS include:

| Scholarship program | Primary eligibility focus |
| --- | --- |
| Juana Aguilar Galang Scholarship | 1st-year priority, with 2nd- and 3rd-year students considered. Prefers CICT, CS, COE, CPTED, and Hagonoy residents. Mandatory in-house interview. |
| Megaworld Foundation | 2nd- to 4th-year students in BS Math (Business Applications), BSBA, or BS Industrial Engineering. Family gross income up to PHP 400,000 per year, GWA of at least 2.25, and no grade below 2.50. |
| Hagonoy and Friends Scholarship Foundation | 1st-year BulSU students residing in Hagonoy, Bulacan. |
| Bounty Cares Foundation | 2nd-year Pulilan residents in BS Industrial Engineering or BS Mechanical Engineering who passed all subjects, endorsed by 2 former professors. |
| Soroptimist International of Makati (LEAP) | Female 3rd- and 4th-year working students with a GWA of at least 2.50 and family gross income up to PHP 80,000 per year. |
| CMEAP Scholarship | 4th-year Civil Engineering students born and residing in Bulacan. |
| Luis Co Chi Kiat Foundation (Puregold) | Financially deserving students across programs, with 2-stage initial and final document screening. |

## Institutional tuition discounts and other national grants

Beyond OSFAS partner calls, BulSU recognizes baseline university discounts and external government grants. Verify current stipend rates directly with OSFAS or the sponsoring agency before applying, since allowances update periodically.

| Discount or grant | Coverage and requirements |
| --- | --- |
| High school valedictorians and salutatorians | 100% 1st-semester tuition discount for valedictorians and 50% for salutatorians; must graduate from a recognized high school with at least 100 graduates certified by your principal |
| Academic scholars | 100% tuition discount for a GPA of **1.50 or better** and 50% for **1.75 or better**; no grade lower than 2.00 across at least 15 academic units over the preceding 2 semesters |
| Student Government officers | Full tuition grant for sitting Student Government Presidents and partial grant for Vice Presidents |
| BulSU employee dependents | 100% tuition discount with no failing grade in any enrolled subject; submit your latest COG, parent's employee ID, and birth certificate |
| Cultural talents and varsity athletes | 50% to 100% tuition discount recommended by advisers, coaches, and Sports Development based on active group service or SCUAA and UCLAA competition service |
| Barangay Scholarship | 100% tuition discount for children of elective barangay officials, excluding tanods |
| External national and local grants | BulSU recognizes DOST, CHED Merit and Grant-in-Aid, ESGP-PA for 4Ps beneficiaries, GSIS, OWWA, PVAO, AFP, and LGU or congressional assistance |

## Screening criteria for university-administered scholarships

University-administered grants and general private benefactors require a baseline GPA of at least 2.00, at least 15 academic units, and no grade of 3.00 unless the donor sets custom terms. OSFAS typically collects your admission files, COG or Form 138, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, electric bill, and Certificate of Good Moral Character.

OSFAS scores applicants on a **100-point rubric**:

| Component | Weight | What OSFAS evaluates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essay test | 20 points | Written reflection on what the scholarship means to you |
| Personality | 20 points | Poise and confidence during the objective test and interview |
| Values | 20 points | Humility, sincerity, truthfulness, and nationalism |
| Reasoning | 20 points | How you plan to use the financial support |
| Economic condition | 20 points | Assessed from utility bills, family size, housing, and parent work |

Once approved and your donor agreement is signed, have OSFAS stamp your scholarship details on your COR before heading to the Accounting Office.

## Scholarship renewal and grade rules

Visit OSFAS for a **renewal form** every semester as soon as your grades are released. Failing to meet grade rules can suspend or terminate your grant:

| Program | Grade retention rule |
| --- | --- |
| Government and congressional grants | Zero failing grades of 5.00 |
| ESGP-PA | Failing more than 2 subjects despite academic intervention terminates the grant |
| Cultural groups | 1 failing mark triggers a warning that you must clear the next semester; 3 failing marks end the scholarship |
| Varsity athletes | Full scholars cannot fail more than 10% of their enrolled units |
