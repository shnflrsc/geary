## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Official Receipt for PHP 100 per set
- Photocopy of your TOR (for records from 2007 and earlier)
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Present your valid ID and TOR photocopy (if applicable) at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
2. Pay PHP 100 per set at the Cashier's Office and keep the Official Receipt.
3. Submit your Official Receipt and TOR photocopy (if applicable) at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
4. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub and valid ID, then sign the release logbook.

## Fee and processing time

A Course Description set costs PHP 100 and takes up to 8 working days to process.
