The Office of the University Registrar issues your Official Transcript of Records (TOR), Certificate of Grades (COG), Certified True Copies (CTC), Certification, Authentication, and Verification (CAV), diploma duplicates, and academic certifications. For a Certificate of Good Moral Character, you go to the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) instead.

All standard requests on the Main Campus are walk-in transactions. You verify your record at the window, pay at the Cashier's Office, submit your receipt, and return with your claim stub on the scheduled release date.

## Fees and processing times

Standard fees and maximum release times on the Main Campus depend on the document and whether your records are already encoded in the student portal (2008 to present) or still in physical archives (2007 and earlier):

| Document requested | Fee | Processing time | Extras needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CTC of Report of Grades or COR | PHP 25 per copy | 1 working day | Photocopy of grades or COR |
| CTC of TOR or Diploma | PHP 25 per copy | 4 working days | Photocopy of TOR or diploma |
| Certification, Authentication, and Verification (CAV) | PHP 50 per copy | 2 working days | Photocopy of TOR (plus diploma if graduated), 1 documentary stamp |
| Certificate of Grades (2008 to present) | PHP 25 per copy | 4 working days | Accomplished Clearance Form (first copy) |
| Certificate of Grades (2007 and earlier) | PHP 25 per copy | 11 working days | Accomplished Clearance Form (first copy) |
| TOR, Not Graduated (2008 to present) | PHP 50 per page | 6 working days | Clearance Form, 1 documentary stamp, school request form if transferring |
| TOR, Not Graduated (2007 and earlier) | PHP 50 per page | 13 working days | Clearance Form, 1 documentary stamp, school request form if transferring |
| TOR, Second Copy Graduated (2008 to present) | PHP 50 per page | 8 working days | 1 documentary stamp, passport photo with name tag if for board exams |
| TOR, Second Copy Graduated (2007 and earlier) | PHP 50 per page | 9 working days | 1 documentary stamp, passport photo with name tag if for board exams |
| Course Description | PHP 100 per set | 8 working days | Photocopy of TOR (for 2007 and earlier) |
| Duplicate Diploma | PHP 300 | 8 working days | Notarized Affidavit of Loss (or damaged original diploma) |
| Second Copy of Form 137 (Laboratory High School) | PHP 50 | 8 working days | LHS Clearance Form (first copy), receiving school request form |
| RLE and Undertaking Certification | PHP 25 per copy | 8 working days | 1 documentary stamp |
| Certifications requiring clearance (Units Earned, CAR, Undergraduate GWA, Undergraduate English Proficiency, Law School Eligibility) | PHP 25 per copy | 4 working days | Accomplished Clearance Form (plus TOR copy for BulSU if for Law School) |
| Certifications not requiring clearance (Enrollment, Graduation, Candidate for Graduation, Grading System, Second Copy Latin Honor, Graduate GWA/English Proficiency, Special-Order Exemption) | PHP 25 per copy | 4 working days | 1 documentary stamp for English Proficiency |
| Certificate of Good Moral Character (OSAS) | PHP 25 per copy | About 5 minutes | OSAS request form, no pending disciplinary case |

Actual release dates can run faster than the maximum turnaround listed above. University advisories also use 2 working days for CAV and simple certifications, 6 working days for recent TORs and most cleared documents, and up to 9 working days for older pre-2008 TORs. Always follow the exact date stamped on your claim stub. By law, schools have an outer limit of 30 days once requirements are complete.

## What to prepare before going to the Registrar

Bring complete requirements on your first visit so you do not lose your place in line:

- One original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Accomplished Clearance Form if your document requires one (get it at the Registrar window or download and print it from the BulSU website)
- Documentary stamp (1 piece per TOR, CAV, RLE, or English Proficiency certificate; available at the BulSU Cashier's Office, Treasury Office, or BIR)
- Supporting attachments specific to your request (such as photocopies of your TOR/COR for CTC, a passport-size photo with name tag on a white background for board-exam TORs, or a notarized Affidavit of Loss for a duplicate diploma)
- If someone else is filing or claiming for you: a signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney, plus one photocopy of your ID and one photocopy of your representative's ID

## Step-by-step walk-in request process

1. **Complete your clearance first (when required).** If you are requesting a TOR (not graduated), a first-copy COG, or a certification requiring clearance, have your Clearance Form signed by five offices: your College Dean's Office, the E-Library, the Accounting Office, OSAS, and the Office of the University Registrar.
2. **Verify your record at the Registrar window.** Present your original valid ID so staff can check your record in the student management system and issue a payment slip with the exact amount due.
3. **Pay at the Cashier's Office.** Present your payment slip at the Cashier and keep the Official Receipt. Buy any required documentary stamps here if available.
4. **Submit your requirements and get your claim stub.** Hand your Official Receipt, Clearance Form (if required), and supporting documents to the designated Registrar window. Check the release date on your claim stub before leaving.
5. **Claim your document on the release date.** Bring your claim stub, valid ID, and required documentary stamps (if not yet handed in) to the Registrar window and sign the release logbook.

## Getting a Certificate of Good Moral Character

Good Moral Character certificates are issued by OSAS, not the Registrar. Main Campus students visit OSAS Main Campus; external campus students visit their campus OSAS office.

1. Fill out the Good Moral Character request form at the OSAS counter. Staff check your name against the university database of active or pending disciplinary cases.
2. Take the billing form to the Cashier's Office and pay PHP 25 per copy.
3. Present your Official Receipt back at OSAS and wait for the Dean's signature. Walk-in release normally takes about five minutes.

## Special cases and record holds

### Freshmen requesting SF-10 (Form 137) from high school

If you are a newly admitted freshman, you still need to submit your SF-10 (formerly Form 137) from your previous high school so your BulSU permanent record stays complete. Get the signed request form from the Registrar and ask your high school to issue your SF-10 with the exact remark `Copy for: Bulacan State University`, then submit it to your campus Registrar before the semester deadline announced on the Registrar's page.

### When the university can hold your records

BulSU can withhold academic records or a degree only if you have an unpaid university balance or an unresolved disciplinary case. Once you settle the balance or the case closes, the hold lifts. If you are a graduating student waiting for your final "Graduated" TOR, make sure you have filed your Application for Graduation and cleared any record discrepancies sent to your email.

### Updating your name in university records

If your legal name needs a correction or update at the Registrar:

- **Married while enrolled:** File an Affidavit of Change of Status together with an authenticated Marriage Contract at the Office of the University Registrar.
- **Mismatch between school records and birth certificate:** Submit a notarized Affidavit of Discrepancy alongside your supporting credentials.
