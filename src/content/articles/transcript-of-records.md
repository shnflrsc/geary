## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment and documentary stamps)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Accomplished Clearance Form signed by your College Dean's Office, E-Library, Accounting Office, OSAS, and Registrar (for non-graduated TORs)
- 1 documentary stamp (available at the Cashier's Office, Treasury Office, or BIR)
- Original school request form remarked `Copy for: [Name of New School]` (if transferring schools)
- Passport-size photo with name tag on a white background (if requesting for board exams)
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Have your Clearance Form signed by the 5 required offices first if you are requesting a non-graduated TOR.
2. Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
3. Pay PHP 50 per page at the Cashier's Office, keep the Official Receipt, and buy a documentary stamp if available.
4. Submit your Official Receipt, Clearance Form (if applicable), and supporting documents at the Registrar's window to get your claim stub.
5. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub, valid ID, and any unsubmitted documentary stamp, then sign the release logbook.

## Fees and processing times

TOR requests cost PHP 50 per page. Maximum processing depends on your graduation status and when your records were filed:

| Record type | Processing time |
| --- | --- |
| Not graduated, 2008 to present | 6 working days |
| Not graduated, 2007 and earlier | 13 working days |
| Graduated second copy, 2008 to present | 8 working days |
| Graduated second copy, 2007 and earlier | 9 working days |

Always follow the release date stamped on your claim stub. By law, schools have an outer limit of 30 days once requirements are complete.

## Record holds and graduating TORs

BulSU can withhold academic records only for unpaid balances or an unresolved disciplinary case. Graduating students waiting for a final "Graduated" TOR must have filed an Application for Graduation and cleared any discrepancies sent to their email.

## High school SF-10 and name updates

- **Freshmen SF-10 / Form 137:** Submit your high school SF-10 remarked `Copy for: Bulacan State University` using the signed Registrar request form before the semester deadline.
- **Name updates:** File an Affidavit of Change of Status with an authenticated Marriage Contract (if married while enrolled), or a notarized Affidavit of Discrepancy with supporting credentials (if your school name differs from your birth certificate), at the Registrar.
