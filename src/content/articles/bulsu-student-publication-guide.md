Campus journalism at BulSU operates under the **Office of Student Publications and Information (OSPI)** in line with the **Campus Journalism Act of 1991 (Republic Act 7079)**. Whether you want to join BulSU's flagship paper or a local college publication, editorial board slots are filled through competitive written exams and panel interviews.

## The Pacesetter and college publications

- ***The Pacesetter*:** BulSU's official university-wide student publication, distributed across the Main Campus and external campuses and funded through the student paper trust fund. Follow [The Pacesetter on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUPacesetter) or email `newsroom.pacesetter@gmail.com` for open calls and coverage inquiries.
- **College and external campus publications:** OSPI also recognizes local student papers across colleges and satellite campuses and coordinates press training workshops.

OSPI safeguards editorial independence and handles formal complaints involving campus publications separately from regular college Student Discipline Committees.

## Editorial board positions

*The Pacesetter* Editorial Board spans **14 core leadership and management positions**:

- **Top leadership:** Editor-in-Chief, Associate Editor, Managing Editor for Administration, and Managing Editor for Finance
- **Section editors:** News, Features, Sports, Literary, and Development Communication (DevComm)
- **Creative and broadcast leads:** Art Director, Online Content Manager, Circulations Manager, Photojournalism Coordinator, and Broadcast Director

## Qualifications to apply

Check that you meet all five requirements before filing an editorial board application:

- Officially enrolled in **at least 15 academic units** (certified by your unit head or the University Registrar)
- Completed **at least 1 year of residence** at BulSU (prior campus publication experience is preferred)
- In **good academic standing** with no failing (`5.00`), incomplete (`INC`), or conditional (`4.00`) mark in any subject
- **Certificate of Good Moral Character** from the Dean of Student Affairs and Services, with zero record of disciplinary action
- **No major position** in any student organization and **no affiliation** with any student political party

## Selection process, term, and removal rules

Applications for the incoming academic year typically open between **March and April** via the **BulSU Student Publications Office** and *The Pacesetter* Facebook pages (with follow-up calls when specific section editor seats remain open).

1. **Written examination and panel interview:** Conducted by an ad hoc selection committee made up of the Head of Student Publications and Information, one faculty member, one jointly accepted media practitioner, and two past editors chosen by the outgoing board (recent screenings ran at Carpio Hall and the Office of Student Publications).
2. **Designation and 1-year term:** Qualifiers are endorsed to the University President for appointment. Each board serves a **1-year term** ending at the close of the academic year, with the next board selected at least **30 days before** the academic year ends.
3. **Removal grounds:** After due process, board members or staff may be removed for incompetence, unauthorized outside affiliation, misuse of publication funds or property, defamation, mocking athletes or persons with disabilities, invading privacy, immorality, or breaking university rules.

## Campus journalism contests and awards

BulSU student journalists regularly train and compete in local, regional, and national press conferences, including *Pandayan Plaridel*, *Gawad Galing Plaridel*, the Regional Tertiary Press Congress, the Regional Higher Education Press Conference (RHEPC), the Annual National CONFAB, and the Luzon-wide Higher Education Press Conference. Outstanding campus writers are also eligible for **Journalist of the Year** at the annual Gold Gear Awards.
