The Office of Student Organizations and Activities (OSOA) and the BulSU-OSO Executive Board accredit and monitor campus organizations. Check the [BulSU OSOA Main Campus Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/osobulsu) each academic year for the official list of Recognized Student Organizations (RSOs), upload portals, and letter templates.

## Join a student organization

Membership in recognized BulSU organizations is voluntary and open to all students, including incoming freshmen. You can join more than 1 organization, subject to each group's constitution and voting rules.

Recognized groups fall into 7 categories: college-based and campus-based academic societies, socio-civic, environmental, lifestyle, political, multi-faith (where OSOA remains faith-neutral), and fraternities.

BulSU limits university-wide recognition to **a maximum of 3 fraternities** at any time. Any fraternity that breaks university rules or the Anti-Hazing Law (RA 8049) faces automatic revocation of recognition and legal action.

Only recognized organizations can carry the BulSU name, reserve campus facilities, collect approved dues, hold events, or sell reasonably priced org merchandise such as Friday org shirts.

## Start or renew a student organization

Every recognized organization must meet 2 standing requirements:

- At least **8 regular enrolled members** in good standing
- Up to 3 full-time BulSU faculty or non-teaching staff advisers serving a renewable 1-year term (part-time faculty with at least 2 consecutive semesters qualify only if no regular employee is available)

### Apply as a new organization

New organizations need **at least 15 founding members**. Submit these documents to OSOA:

- Letter of intent and College Dean endorsement
- Recognition application form, Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Adviser profile, adviser invitation, and signed acceptance letter
- Officer and member lists with signatures, plus signed voluntary membership forms (lists alone suffice for 50 or more members)
- Annual activity proposal and budget proposal

### Renew an existing organization

Annual renewal opens around June to September. Submit these documents to OSOA:

- Renewal application form, updated Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Updated officer and membership lists, plus adviser profile
- Activity proposal and budget proposal for the incoming year
- Audited financial report with official receipts or invoices and photocopies of thermal receipts
- Accomplishment report with photos and narrative documentation, plus Certificate of Completion

### Review timeline, appeals, and awards

OSOA notifies applicants within **2 weeks**. If recognition is denied, you have **10 days** to appeal in writing to the Dean of the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS), who decides within **10 working days**.

Recognized organizations receive their certificates during *Balangayan sa Unibersidad* and compete for annual honors: Outstanding Student Organization, Outstanding Student Leader, and Outstanding Adviser.

## Get approval for organization activities

Every event requires the organization president's and adviser's signatures before the activity and a narrative report afterward. Sports and team-building events also require written safety guidelines.

### In-campus face-to-face events

Submit an activity letter, proposal form, budget form, venue letter, photocopy of the adviser's ID, and organizer list. Add parental consent forms if the event runs outside school hours.

In-campus events require a **1:50** faculty or adviser companion ratio. Approvals go up to the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA), or the Campus Administrator on external campuses.

### Off-campus face-to-face events

Submit all in-campus documents, plus a Compliance Checklist, organizer and participant lists, invitation letters, Commission on Higher Education (CHED) endorsement, OSOA certification, and parental consent forms.

Off-campus events require a **1:20** faculty companion ratio. Papers route through OSOA, the Office of Student Development (OSD), the OSAS Dean, and the VPAA to the University President.

### Online activities

Submit an activity letter, proposal form, and organizer and participant list to OSOA for approval. Add a program flow, competition rules, or donation-drive action plan when applicable.

## Rooms, fundraising, and campus posters

### Reserve a campus room

Check availability with the facility head first, then submit your room request **at least 1 week ahead**. Regular classes always take priority, and one organization cannot book a room on another group's behalf.

### Run a fundraising activity

Organizations may hold **1 major fundraising activity per academic year**, approved by the University President. Off-campus solicitation also requires a Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) permit. Submit a financial report to OSOA within **2 weeks** after the activity.

### Post campus posters and flyers

File a signed letter of intent with the original poster at OSOA, plus College Dean approval for college buildings. Mount posters only on designated bulletin boards using scotch tape, masking tape, or push pins for **at most 2 weeks**, and remove them the day after expiration.
