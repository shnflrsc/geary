These food safety, housing, security, and disaster drill rules apply across the Malolos Main Campus, the external campuses in Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, Sarmiento, and San Rafael, and the Plaridel Extension.

## University canteen and dining rules

The Office of Food Services supervises both the University Canteen and private concessionaires under Department of Health standards. Food handlers complete annual medical exams, and stalls undergo random food safety sampling alongside price and nutrition checks.

The University Canteen and Dining Hall runs on self-service. Follow these dining rules:

- Return soiled trays and bottles to the designated tray area and dispose of trash properly.
- Keep noise down inside the dining area.
- Vacate tables promptly during peak breakfast and lunch hours so other students can eat.

## Campus housing and accredited boarding houses

### Accredited off-campus boarding houses

If you commute from outside Malolos, ask Student Welfare about boarding houses inspected by BulSU's Housing Accrediting Committee for ventilation, lighting, clean beds, water supply, and safety.

### Athletes' Dormitories

The Sports Development Office supervises the Athletes' Dormitories for qualified varsity athletes. BulSU assumes no liability for lost, stolen, or damaged personal items.

### BulSU Hostel

The BulSU Hostel has short-stay rooms and function halls on campus, with a **20% discount** for BulSU students, employees, and alumni. Confirm current room rates and booking requirements directly with the Hostel office before checking in.

While BulSU respects residents' privacy, authorized staff may enter rooms for:

- Emergencies threatening health or property
- Resident permission or search warrants
- Official duties, maintenance, or noise control
- Checkout, semester breaks, and Christmas breaks

## Campus security and disaster drills

Licensed security guards man every university gate to enforce **No ID, No Entry**, manage campus traffic, spot hazards, and issue violation slips.

During emergencies and drills, the Office of Safety, Security, and Disaster Risk Management (OSSDRRM) and the BulSU Action Team (BulSUAcT) coordinate with Malolos authorities, the Philippine National Police (PNP), and the Bureau of Fire Protection.

BulSU joins the quarterly Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED), announced on [BulSU OSSDRRM on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ossdrrm) and the main BulSU Facebook page.

### Respond to an earthquake

1. Duck, cover, and hold as soon as the alarm sounds or shaking starts. Get under a sturdy desk or table, hold 1 leg, and shield your head and eyes away from windows and tall shelves.
2. Wait until the shaking stops, or until the 1-minute drill bell finishes. Check yourself and classmates for injuries and watch for electrical or gas hazards.
3. Evacuate via stairs only and never use elevators. Move calmly to designated open assembly areas away from buildings, trees, streetlights, power lines, and building entrances where debris can fall.

### Evacuate during a fire

#### Inside the building

1. Stay calm and alert your college response team.
2. Pull the nearest fire alarm and notify campus security.
3. Exit through marked fire exits without locking doors behind you.
4. Help classmates with disabilities (PWDs) evacuate safely.

#### Outside the building

- Leave interior fire alarms untouched.
- Report your name and the exact fire location to security immediately.
- Keep access roads clear for responders.
