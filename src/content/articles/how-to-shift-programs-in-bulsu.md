Enrolled BulSU students who want to switch courses apply through the combined **Shifters, Transferees, and Second-Degree Coursers** track. **Never apply through the Freshman Admission portal**, because shifters caught using Freshman Admission are barred from all BulSU programs.

## Eligibility, schedule, and residency limits

Shifting covers:

- **Main Campus shifters:** incoming 2nd-year or 3rd-year students
- **External campus shifters:** incoming 3rd-year students

Voluntary shifters need satisfactory academic standing and an open slot in the receiving program. Shifting does not reset your maximum residency clock from initial entry (6 years for a 4-year course, 7.5 years for a 5-year course), so switching late can affect how many semesters you have left.

### Recent application window (A.Y. 2026-2027 reference)

During the May 2026 cycle, online applications ran May 20 to 29, 2026 (extended May 27 to June 1, 2026) and hard-copy appointments ran May 21 to 30, 2026 (extended May 28 to June 2, excluding May 25-26, Sundays, and holidays) at Malolos Campus (temporarily at BulSU E-Library 2nd Floor). Only limited slots open each year.

On-site document submission for shifters has **no fee**. Confirm on the current cycle announcement if any college testing fee applies.

## When you must take an exam

Whether you need an exam depends on the kind of move you are making:

- Shifting between different degree programs (such as Education to Engineering, or Engineering to Architecture): Shifter's Examination required.
- Shifting from an external BulSU campus: Shifter's Examination required.
- Continuing from a ladderized program toward a bachelor's degree: continuing examination required.
- Changing majors within the same degree program: exempted from the Shifter's Exam.

For reference, Sarmiento Campus held its A.Y. 2026-2027 Shifters, Transferees, and Second-Degree Admission Test on June 5, 2026.

## Documents to prepare

Prepare scanned copies for online filing and matching physical copies for your Malolos Campus appointment:

- Letter of intent
- Certified copy of grades (Certificate of Grades or Transcript of Records)
- Clearance
- Original and photocopy of your current Certificate of Registration (COR) and class cards

## Application steps

1. Watch the [BulSU Admissions Office Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice) for the Shifters, Transferees, and Second-Degree Coursers list of open programs across Main Campus and external campuses (Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, San Rafael, and Sarmiento).
2. Submit your online application using the portal link announced for that window.
3. Bring your physical requirements to the University Admissions Office at Malolos Campus (temporarily at BulSU E-Library 2nd Floor in May 2026) on your scheduled appointment date. Validation takes about 8 minutes.
4. Watch your registered email for confirmation and your tentative exam schedule (if your move requires testing).
5. Complete the mandatory annual physical, medical, and dental examination at University Health Services before enrolling in your new program.

## Shifting after academic delinquency

BulSU also allows or recommends shifting when your grades drop under Academic Delinquency rules:

- Failing or dropping two subjects while on probation drops you from your current course, though you may apply to shift with acceptance from the receiving Dean and approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA).
- Failing 50% to 75% of your enrolled subjects triggers a recommendation to shift to another program, subject to the receiving Dean's acceptance and VPAA approval.

Failing more than 75% of your enrolled subjects in one semester (or 50% or more while on probation) disqualifies you from the university.
