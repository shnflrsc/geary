## Mandatory graduation requirements

Beyond passing every subject in your curriculum, every candidate for graduation must complete 4 requirements:

- File an Application for Graduation at the Office of the University Registrar once you finish your program requirements. For timing reference, June 2026 graduates had a June 15, 2026 cutoff, and late filings receive the lowest priority for Transcript of Records (TOR) processing.
- Complete at least **1 year of residence** at BulSU before graduation (Latin Honors require 2 years of residency).
- Clear all financial and property accountabilities with the library, laboratories, student organizations, and Cashier before commencement using your campus Registrar's current Student Clearance Form.
- Resolve all pending disciplinary charges and serve any sanctions in full before graduation.

## Avoid final-semester delays

### Clear INC and 4.00 grades before the semester deadline

Any open INC or 4.00 blocks graduation clearance and converts to **5.00** if you miss the Registrar's semester completion deadline. BulSU does not offer a separate graduation-only INC extension, so follow the regular Registrar completion dates.

### Petition for a tutorial class if 1 subject is unoffered

Graduating seniors blocked by a single unoffered course can petition for a Tutorial Class. Secure a graduating-student certification from the Office of the University Registrar, get your Dean's endorsement, and obtain approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs for Main Campus students or the Vice President for Executive Operations for external campus students.

## Documents to claim your "Graduated" TOR

Your Registrar releases a TOR marked "Graduated" only after you finish all graduation requirements and file your Application for Graduation. Confirm these mandatory records are complete in your student file:

- School Form 10 (Form 137)
- Original School Form 9 (Grade 12 Report Card)
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Birth Certificate

Transferees must also have their Honorable Dismissal and official TOR copy for BulSU on file.

If your TOR cannot be printed because of record discrepancies, watch your email for resolution instructions from your campus Registrar.

## Commencement exercises and the Loyalty Award

Candidates are expected to attend commencement exercises on dates set by the Academic Council and wear the prescribed academic costume for both baccalaureate and commencement ceremonies. Confirm your college's final commencement venue on [Bulacan State University's Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bulsuofficial) before traveling, and confirm current graduation fees directly with your college or Registrar.

BulSU gives the Loyalty Award at graduation to students who started Grade 7 at BulSU Laboratory High School and completed their bachelor's degree continuously at BulSU.
