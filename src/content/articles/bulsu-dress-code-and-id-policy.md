Campus security enforces uniform and ID rules at every BulSU gate.

## Weekly uniform schedule

Across BulSU colleges and external campuses, the standard school week follows this schedule:

| Day or class | Prescribed attire | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday, Tuesday, Thursday | Prescribed university uniform | Wear black shoes, a visible BulSU ID lanyard, and your required BulSU or college pin |
| Wednesday | Wash Day | Clean, decent civilian clothing |
| Friday | Organization or college shirt | Recognized org or college shirt; your prescribed daily uniform also works |
| Saturday | Wash Day or proper civilian | Varies by college or campus; some allow Wash Day while others require smart casual |
| PATHFIT, PE, and NSTP | Prescribed activity uniform | PATHFIT shirt, shorts, and rubber shoes worn only during those class hours |
| Laboratory classes | Prescribed lab outfit | Required for safety inside laboratory rooms |

### Opening-of-year grace period

Freshmen typically get about 1 month of civilian clothing at the start of a new academic year while uniforms are being prepared. 2nd- to 5th-year students shift to mandatory uniform wearing right after opening week. Attire must stay clean and decent even on civilian days and during grace periods.

## Uniform variations by college and campus

Most Main Campus colleges use a white short-sleeved blouse or polo, black pants or skirt, black shoes, a BulSU or college pin, and a black necktie for women. Exact details vary by college and campus:

- Engineering, CBEA, and CSER require their own collar pins and tie styles.
- Sarmiento general programs and Bustos Teacher Education use blue slacks or skirts.
- Hospitality Management uses a white chef-style polo, while Tourism Management and Food Processing Technology follow program-specific uniform and footwear rules.

Check your college or campus Local Student Council (LSC) uniform advisory before buying sets for a new semester.

## Hair, jewelry, and grooming rules

- Hair color is limited to black, blond, and brown shades. Cultural performers are exempted only during official competitions or stage performances that require it.
- Male students with long hair must keep it neatly fixed. Clinical, hospitality, criminology, and ROTC-related programs may enforce stricter haircut standards.
- Male students cannot wear earrings on campus, and female students are advised to avoid excessive jewelry for personal safety.
- Cross-dressing is not permitted during regular campus days. Exceptions apply only to approved social events such as College Night and the Students' Ball.

## No ID, No Entry gate policy

Wear your BulSU ID visibly whenever you enter or walk around campus. Only 2 temporary entry options work at the gate.

### If you left your ID at home

Present your current Certificate of Registration (COR) to the gate guard. The guard writes a remark, dates it, and signs over their printed name on the back of your COR before letting you enter.

### If you lost your ID

Report it immediately to the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) and the Office of the University Registrar. A notarized Affidavit of Loss counts as temporary gate entry for **only 3 days** while you process a replacement card.

Never lend, alter, or swap photos on a BulSU ID. Security guards confiscate tampered IDs on the spot and escort you straight to your College Dean for disciplinary action.
