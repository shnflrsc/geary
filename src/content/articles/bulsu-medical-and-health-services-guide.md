Enrolled BulSU students receive two built-in health protections: free walk-in care and medical certificates at University Health Services, and 24/7 Enhanced Student Accident Insurance processed through the Office of Student Welfare (OSW).

## University Health Services clinic hours and care

Main Campus Health Services is open **Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM**. During peak freshman enrollment periods, medical and dental exams may move to temporary campus venues such as Valencia Hall. Check [BulSU Health Services Main Campus](https://www.facebook.com/bulsuclinicmain) or Admissions posts before lining up.

The clinic is staffed by a University Physician, a dentist, and nurses. Enrolled students get free access to:

- Medical and dental consultations, basic medicines when available, oral prophylaxis (cleaning), and tooth extraction
- Trauma first aid, emergency ambulance transport when needed, vaccination drives, and health education
- Admission medical and dental exams for freshmen, transferees, and shifters

For admission medical and dental exams, bring:

- Completed Medical and Dental Assessment Form
- Chest X-ray taken within the last **6 months**
- Photocopy of medical clearance, if applicable
- PWD ID, if applicable

An illness certified by the University Physician counts as an excused absence, up to the 30% semester cap. If the University Physician formally advises you to stop attending for health reasons, you qualify for a full refund of paid tuition.

## Getting free medical and dental certificates

All standard student medical and dental certificates at University Health Services are **free**.

### Absent student excuse (about 6 minutes)

- Original BulSU ID or Certificate of Registration (COR)
- Parent or guardian excuse letter with wet signature for 1 to 2 days absent, or a licensed physician's medical certificate for 3 or more days absent
- Photocopy or softcopy of your parent or guardian's valid government ID

### OJT, Practice Teaching, or Field Study (about 14 minutes)

- CARDSIS endorsement and intern list, forwarded by CARDSIS
- Original BulSU ID or COR
- Photocopy or softcopy of a chest X-ray valid within 6 months

### Seminars, field trips, or school activities (about 14 minutes)

- Approved activity letter and participant list from your college coordinator
- Original BulSU ID or COR

For OJT and off-campus activity certificates, you go through a brief health assessment on site. If the physician or dentist flags a concern, you will need to submit an outside clearance before the certificate is released.

## 24/7 Enhanced Student Accident Insurance

Every bona fide enrolled BulSU student is covered **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, on or off campus, for personal accidents, road accidents, and animal or insect bites. Injuries resulting from self-harm or intoxication are excluded.

Meet both filing deadlines:

1. **Report the incident within 30 days** at the Office of Student Welfare (OSW) to get your official claim form and case-specific checklist.
2. **Submit complete requirements within 90 days** from the date of the accident or death.

### Standard requirements for an accident claim

- Accomplished Accident Insurance Claim Form from OSW
- One original Incident Report signed by you
- One original Medical Certificate from your attending physician
- Original official receipts for hospitalization, medicines, laboratory tests, radiology, and accident-related procedures
- Original Hospital Statement of Account showing admission and discharge dates if confined, plus original medicine prescriptions
- Original and photocopy of your BulSU ID, plus one original COR
- Photo of the accident or injury

### Additional requirements by incident type

- **Animal or insect bite:** Vaccination Certificate and a copy of your anti-rabies vaccination record.
- **Vehicular accident:** Original Police Report, plus original and photocopy of an updated Driver's License if you were driving.

### Requirements for accidental death claims

Beneficiaries must submit:

- Accomplished Accident Insurance Claim Form from OSW
- Original PSA Death Certificate
- Original proof of relationship, such as a PSA birth or marriage certificate
- Student's BulSU ID and original COR
- Original Autopsy or Medico-Legal Report
- Original Police Report and incident photos
- Witness affidavit, if applicable
- Updated Driver's License, if the student was driving

Once you turn in complete documents at OSW, counter verification and forwarding to the insurance provider take about **15 minutes**. The provider contacts you and sends an SMS when your reimbursement cheque is ready for pickup.
