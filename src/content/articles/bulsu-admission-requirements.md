**If you have already enrolled in another college or another BulSU program, do not apply as a freshman.** Transferees and shifters caught using Freshman Admission are barred from all BulSU programs.

Freshman admission depends on your Grade 11 grades, Senior High School (SHS) strand alignment, required documents, Admission Test for BulSU (ATBulSU) score, and optional Affirmative Action records.

## Who can apply as a freshman

You qualify for Freshman Admission if you meet all of these conditions:

- You are an SHS candidate for graduation or an SHS graduate from a public school, a Department of Education (DepEd)-recognized private school, or an accredited secondary school abroad.
- You have never enrolled in or attended any college or university before the academic year you are applying for.

Alternative Learning System (ALS) graduates with an Accreditation and Equivalency (A&E) Assessment and Certification also appear under BulSU's freshman Affirmative Action categories.

## Grade 11 GWA and subject cutoffs

Your Grade 11 School Form 9 (Form 138) sets which programs you can select:

| Program group | Minimum Grade 11 GWA | Subject or track requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board and licensure programs | 87% | At least 85% in all subjects |
| Engineering, Architecture, Accountancy | 87% | At least 87% in Math and Science |
| BS Real Estate Management and BS Customs Administration (new for A.Y. 2027-2028) | 87% | At least 85% in all subjects and 87% in Math and Science |
| Secondary Education (BSEd) | 87% | At least 87% in your major subject, such as Math for BSEd Math |
| Nursing, Psychology, Medical Technology | 87% | At least 87% in Science |
| Non-board programs | 85% | None beyond the 85% overall GWA |
| College of Industrial Technology (CIT) | No fixed GWA | Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) track graduates with NC I or NC II preferred |
| College of Sports, Exercise, and Recreation (CSER) | No fixed GWA | Sports track applicants and provincial, regional, or national athletes preferred |

For any program leading to a licensure examination, you must also score **80% or higher** on the admission exam.

Verify which campuses offer your chosen course on the [A.Y. 2027-2028 Programs Offered post](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice/posts/pfbid0g7pPsC6Wy1jXtAXzy1XtkqMAqZuF92pDP2eXSVGWMi68RT1U5x25VykfcVKTDhJtl) and the [September 25, 2026 new programs announcement](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice/posts/pfbid02wGRhrRnepE3X4YoqNmgbD159M5CvRRnBf3rNtTYc8JRKFWtpxEakByPCPZM6jSJRl). On the official program graphics, red marks board programs, black marks non-board programs, and blue marks newly added programs.

## Mandatory documents to prepare

Upload scanned copies on the [BulSU Online Application Portal](https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/), then bring matching physical copies on your scheduled appointment date. On-site document submission for new college students has **no fee** and takes about 8 minutes.

- Certified True Copy of your Grade 11 Report Card (School Form 9, covering both 1st and 2nd semesters) with school seal
- Two originally signed copies of the Application Form (Declaration Form), emailed after online application, each with a 2" x 2" ID photo with name tag on a white background
- Certified True Copy or photocopy of Proof of Residency from your barangay
- Photocopy of a recent electric, water, or internet utility bill, or a Barangay Certification if you cannot present a utility bill
- Photocopy of a recent Income Tax Return (ITR), Certificate of Tax Exemption, or notarized Barangay Certification of low or no income

## Optional documents for Affirmative Action

Affirmative Action accounts for up to **20 points** (20% of your final selection score). Submit only the documents that apply to you.

### Residency (up to 3 points)

- **Bulacan permanent resident (3 points):** Barangay Certification
- **Other Region 3 province permanent resident (2 points):** Barangay Certification

### Group A (3 points, submit at most one)

| Category | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- |
| Indigenous Cultural Community or Indigenous People (ICC/IP) | National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) certificate, or tribal or barangay certificate |
| Pantawid Pamilya Pilipino Program (4Ps) beneficiary | 4Ps ID or barangay certification |
| Student with Special Needs (SSN) or disability under RA 7277 | PWD ID or membership certificate |
| Alternative Learning System (ALS) graduate | Accreditation and Equivalency (A&E) Assessment and Certification |

### Group B (3 points, submit at most one)

| Category | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- |
| Child of a solo parent | Solo Parent ID from the City or Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO or MSWDO) |
| Solo parent under RA 8972 | Birth certificate of your child |
| Single or double orphan living with relatives | Death certificate of one or both parents |
| Orphaned or abandoned student living in an orphanage | Certification from a Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)-recognized institution |
| First-generation college student (no parent or legal guardian finished college) | CSWDO or MSWDO certification, Affidavit of Non-Graduation, or barangay or school certification (confirm the required format with UAO) |

### Additional categories

| Category | Points | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Area (GIDA) resident | 3 | Barangay or local government unit (LGU) certification |
| Exemplary artistic or athletic ability | 3 | Certification from your school head or community, civic, or religious organization head stating your title and award |
| BulSU Adopted Community member or child of a BulSU employee with at least 1 year of service | 5 | Certificate from the University Extension Office or your parent's latest BulSU Employee ID |

Working students with a Certificate of Employment appeared as an optional category in the 2026 cycle, though it is not listed in the current Affirmative Action points table.
