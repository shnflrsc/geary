The Admission Test for Bulacan State University (ATBulSU) is a free, single-day, paper-based entrance exam for incoming college freshmen. BulSU runs the exam on the same day across the Malolos Main Campus and all satellite campuses: Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, San Rafael, and Sarmiento (with external campus updates also covering Plaridel Extension).

## Eligibility, fees, and course rules

- Taking ATBulSU is **free**. BulSU charges no application or exam fee.
- Only incoming freshmen may apply. You qualify if you are a Senior High School (SHS) candidate for graduation, an SHS graduate from a public or DepEd-recognized private school, a graduate of an accredited secondary school abroad, or an Alternative Learning System (ALS) graduate with Accreditation and Equivalency (A&E) certification.
- You must never have enrolled in or attended any college or university before the academic year you apply for. Transferees, shifters, and second-degree applicants follow a separate admission track.
- You get **2 course choices only**: a 1st-choice program and an allied 2nd-choice program. Choices lock once the Admissions Office stamps your hard copies, and you cannot switch programs after results come out. Admitted freshmen must finish 1 full academic year before applying to shift.
- BS Nursing and the College of Nursing are no longer listed under Main Campus in the [A.Y. 2027-2028 Programs Offered post](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice/posts/pfbid0g7pPsC6Wy1jXtAXzy1XtkqMAqZuF92pDP2eXSVGWMi68RT1U5x25VykfcVKTDhJtl). Apply through San Rafael Campus if you want BS Nursing.

### Grounds for disqualification

BulSU voids your application if you commit any of these violations:

- Applying as a freshman when you already have college units (**results in blacklisting across all BulSU programs**)
- Submitting multiple applications
- Giving false details on the portal or falsifying the Declaration Form
- Breaking exam-day rules
- Missing your scheduled hard-copy submission date

### Apply online and validate your documents

1. Open the [BulSU Admissions Portal](https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/) and select the Freshmen form using an active email address with free storage.
2. Fill out all 7 sections of the form (personal details, educational background, application details, family background, medical records, medical history, and supporting documents) and upload your scanned files.
3. Pick your 1st Choice Program and an allied 2nd Choice Program. If you miss the cutoff for your 1st choice, the University Admissions Office (UAO) evaluates you for your 2nd choice automatically.
4. Download the Declaration Form sent to your email, print 2 copies, sign them, and attach a 2" x 2" white-background ID photo with name tag to each copy.
5. Book an on-site appointment and bring your hard copies to your target campus's Admissions Office. Validation takes 5 to 8 minutes. On Main Campus, the office sits beside the College of Law Building and moves temporarily to the 2nd Floor of the University E-Library during peak periods.
6. Wait for your emailed ATBulSU Test Permit, which lists your permit number, exam date, batch time, testing center, building, room assignment, and QR code.
7. Print your test permit on short bond paper. Some satellite campuses, including Sarmiento, ask examinees to bring 2 copies: 1 inside the clear envelope and 1 to show the proctor.

The same portal also hosts separate forms for graduate programs, Expanded Tertiary Education Equivalency and Accreditation Program (ETEEAP), Night Class, and certificate programs.

## Documents to upload and bring

Upload your 2" x 2" white-background ID photo (with printed name tag and no computer-generated edits) as a `.jpeg` file, and upload your Grade 11 Report Card as a `.pdf` file. BulSU has not published a file-size limit for the portal.

Bring hard copies matching your uploads to your on-site validation appointment:

- Grade 11 Report Card (SF9 / Form 138): certified true copy with school seal covering both 1st and 2nd semesters. Form 137 (SF10) and Certification of Enrollment are not required for freshman filing.
- Declaration Form (2 originally signed copies): printed from your portal email, each with a 2" x 2" white-background photo with name tag.
- Proof of Residency: certified true copy or photocopy issued by your barangay.
- Utility bill: photocopy of a recent electric, water, or internet bill (or a Barangay Certification if you cannot provide one).
- Proof of income: photocopy of a recent Income Tax Return (ITR), Certificate of Tax Exemption, or notarized Barangay Certification of low or no income.

### Optional Affirmative Action documents

Select an Affirmative Action category on the portal only if you already have the matching proof. Unverified claims can flag your application during validation.

#### Group A: Priority demographic group (3 points, submit at most 1)

| Category | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- |
| Indigenous Cultural Communities or Indigenous Peoples (ICC/IP) | NCIP, tribal, or barangay certification |
| Pantawid Pamilya Pilipino Program (4Ps) beneficiary | 4Ps ID or barangay certification |
| Student with Special Needs (SSN) or Person with Disability (PWD) | SWD or barangay PWD ID, or membership certificate |
| Alternative Learning System (ALS) graduate | Accreditation and Equivalency (A&E) Assessment and Certification |

#### Group B: Family and social support group (3 points, submit at most 1)

| Category | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- |
| Child of a solo parent | CSWDO or MSWDO Solo Parent ID |
| Solo parent | Child's birth certificate |
| Orphan living with relatives | Death certificate of 1 or both parents |
| Orphaned or abandoned student in a shelter | Certification from a DSWD-recognized orphanage or shelter |
| First-generation college student | See discrepancy note below |

> **Discrepancy on First-Generation College Student proof:** Official BulSU documents disagree on what counts as valid proof. Board of Regents Resolution No. 213, s. 2025 requires an *Affidavit of Non-Graduation*, while the Admissions Office checklist asks for a *CSWDO or MSWDO certification* (and the 2027-2028 guidelines also mention barangay or school certification). Confirm with UAO before uploading so your claim is not rejected.

#### Additional Affirmative Action categories (submit any that apply)

| Category | Points | Accepted proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Area (GIDA) resident | 3 | Barangay or LGU certification |
| Exemplary artistic or athletic ability | 3 | Certificate from your school head or a community, civic, or religious organization head naming your title and award |
| BulSU Adopted Community member or child of a BulSU employee (at least 1 year of service) | 5 | University Extension Office certificate or parent's latest BulSU Employee ID |

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) NC I or NC II certificates are preferred for College of Industrial Technology applicants. Working students with a Certificate of Employment appeared as an optional category in the 2026 cycle, though it is not listed in the 2027-2028 points table.

### Items to bring on exam day

Pack all exam-day items inside 1 **short transparent plastic envelope**:

- ATBulSU Test Permit printed on short bond paper with a visible QR code
- 1 valid government or school photo ID
- 2 black ballpoint pens (gel pens and rollerball pens are prohibited)
- Small bottle of alcohol in a transparent container
- Plain water in a transparent bottle only

## Exam schedule, venue, and exam-day rules

All campuses hold ATBulSU on the same Sunday in 2 batches. Based on coverage by *The Communiqué*, BulSU's College of Arts and Letters student publication, the morning batch runs from 8:15 AM to 10:15 AM and the afternoon batch runs from 1:15 PM to 3:15 PM.

Lines start much earlier than the test hour. ATBulSU 2026 coverage noted examinees queuing by 6:00 AM.

- Arrive **at least 1 hour early**. BulSU has not published an exact late-arrival cutoff, and traffic approaching Malolos Main Campus Gate 1 can back up by up to 2 hours on exam day.
- Testing centers are computer-generated and non-transferable. You cannot switch batches or reschedule for conflicts with other entrance exams. If your testing center is on a different campus than your chosen program, you will still enroll at your chosen campus if you qualify.
- Wear a plain **white shirt, polo, or blouse**. Colored tops were barred in the 2026 advisory.
- Only examinees may enter the campus. Parents, guardians, and chaperones must wait outside the gates, except verified companions assisting PWD examinees. External campuses restrict entry to foot traffic.
- BulSU provides **no baggage storage** and accepts no liability for items left outside. Student publications reported piles of unattended bags, ₱50 external storage fees, and missing items in 2025 and 2026. Leave bags and valuables at home and keep fare or cash inside your transparent envelope.

### Prohibited items

Having any of these items inside the testing room once the exam starts is treated as cheating:

- Electronic devices of any kind (cellphones, calculators, cameras, e-cigarettes, tablets, music players, AirPods), even if turned off
- **All watches and fitness trackers**, including analog wristwatches
- Jewelry, accessories, headsets, headphones, and headwear covering the ears (religious headwear such as a hijab is exempted)
- Bags, wallets, and valuables
- Food and candies
- **Outside scratch paper** (BulSU provides scratch paper during the test)

Proctors inspect pockets and face masks. Keep your eyes and ears visible throughout the test.

### Request PWD testing accommodations

Email **admissions@bulsu.edu.ph** with `PWD-ASSISTANCE` in the subject line (for example, `PWD-ASSISTANCE 2027`). State the help you need and attach your PWD ID or certification.

## Exam coverage and preparation

BulSU does not publish an official syllabus, item count, per-subtest timer, or right-minus-wrong policy. Officially, ATBulSU is a roughly 2-hour paper-based optical mark recognition (OMR) exam where you shade circles on an answer sheet. Calculators are banned, and board or licensure programs require an exam score of at least 80%.

Examinees from 2024 to 2026 consistently report around 200 multiple-choice items in 2 hours, or about 36 seconds per item. Several takers noted answer sheets printed up to 300 numbers even though the test had 200 items, with a 100-item General Knowledge section at the back. Multiple 2024 and 2025 examinees also confirmed there was no Abstract Reasoning section.

Reported subtests cover 4 areas:

- Mathematics: SHS STEM and ABM topics covering Statistics and Probability, manual Business Math computations, Trigonometry, Algebra, Permutations and Combinations, Sets, and Differential Calculus.
- Science: Applied SHS STEM covering Earth Science (rocks, plate tectonics, volcanoes), Physical Science, Biology (photosynthesis, plant anatomy), Basic Chemistry (periodic table), and Basic Physics formulas.
- Language: English and Filipino grammar, vocabulary, parts of speech, figures of speech, and reading comprehension.
- General Knowledge: 100 items placed at the back of the booklet.

### Pacing tips from passers

- Start with your strongest section or the General Knowledge items at the back so manual Math computations do not eat up your time early.
- Skip difficult items right away and come back if you have time left.
- Shade an answer for every item; do not leave blanks.

## Scoring, grade cutoffs, and results

### Grade 11 minimum requirements

| Program category | Minimum Grade 11 GWA | Additional requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board or licensure programs | 87% | At least 85% in every Grade 11 subject, plus at least 80% on ATBulSU |
| Engineering, Architecture, Accountancy | 87% | At least 87% in Math and Science |
| BS Real Estate Management and BS Customs Administration (new for A.Y. 2027-2028) | 87% | At least 85% in all subjects and 87% in Math and Science |
| Secondary Education (BSEd) | 87% | At least 87% in your major subject |
| Nursing, Psychology, Medical Technology | 87% | At least 87% in Science |
| Non-board programs | 85% | None |
| College of Industrial Technology (CIT) | No strict floor | TVL track with relevant NC I or NC II preferred |
| College of Sports, Exercise, and Recreation (CSER) | No strict floor | Sports track and provincial, regional, or national athletes preferred |

Confirm campus availability on the [A.Y. 2027-2028 Programs Offered post](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice/posts/pfbid0g7pPsC6Wy1jXtAXzy1XtkqMAqZuF92pDP2eXSVGWMi68RT1U5x25VykfcVKTDhJtl) and the [September 25, 2026 new board programs announcement](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice/posts/pfbid02wGRhrRnepE3X4YoqNmgbD159M5CvRRnBf3rNtTYc8JRKFWtpxEakByPCPZM6jSJRl). On official program graphics, red marks board programs, black marks non-board programs, and blue marks newly added programs.

### Composite scoring formula

BulSU ranks qualifiers on a 100-point composite score:

| Criterion | Standard | With interview | Engineering |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATBulSU exam | 50% | 50% | 40% |
| College of Engineering exam | Not applicable | Not applicable | 20% |
| Grade 11 GWA | 15% | 10% | 10% |
| SHS strand alignment | 15% | 10% | 10% |
| College interview | Not applicable | 10% | Not applicable |
| Affirmative Action | 20% | 20% | 20% |

SHS strand alignment points depend on how closely your strand matches your chosen program:

| Strand match | Standard | With interview | Engineering |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Directly related (STEM for Engineering) | 15 pts | 10 pts | 10 pts |
| Allied (GAS for Engineering) | 10 pts | 7 pts | 5 pts |
| Not related (Other strands for Engineering) | 5 pts | 5 pts | 2 pts |

Ask UAO for exact strand-to-program mappings, since the strand appendix is omitted from the published guidelines PDF.

Affirmative Action accounts for up to 20 points across 6 categories under the A.Y. 2027-2028 guidelines:

| Affirmative Action category | Max points |
| --- | --- |
| Residency (3 pts for Bulacan residents, 2 pts for other Region 3 provinces) | 3 |
| Priority Demographic Group / Group A (max 1 counted) | 3 |
| Family and Social Support Group / Group B (max 1 counted) | 3 |
| GIDA resident | 3 |
| Exemplary artistic or athletic ability | 3 |
| BulSU Adopted Community or child of a BulSU employee | 5 |

Earlier BOR Resolution No. 213, s. 2025 had an arithmetic mismatch where it printed *Subtotal: 20 pts* while capping Residency at 2 points (summing to 19). The 2027-2028 guidelines raised Residency to 3 points so the 6 categories total 20.

UAO notifies shortlisted applicants who need college interviews, skill tests, or the College of Engineering specialization exam.

### Results, waitlist batches, and anti-fixer warning

Results come out roughly 6 to 9 weeks after exam day. The 2025 results took 61 days, while the 2026 results took 47 days. In 2026, about 10,600 out of 31,000 examinees qualified, a 34.19% passing rate.

BulSU posts alphabetical PDF Lists of Qualifiers by college and program on the [BulSU official website](https://bulsu.edu.ph/) and shares links on the BulSU Official and BulSU Admissions Office Facebook pages. If your name is not on the list, you did not qualify in the initial release.

BulSU has no written waitlist or reconsideration rubric. Once initial qualifiers miss their slot-reservation deadlines, UAO automatically emails ranked non-qualifiers in batches from May through July or August. You do not file a separate reconsideration form. These emails give a tight or single-day reporting window where missing the date forfeits the slot, and you cannot change the offered program.

Anyone offering to help non-qualifiers get admitted is unauthorized. Do not pay fixers or rely on endorsement letters.

## Slot reservation, enrollment, and orientation

Qualifiers must complete on-site slot reservation on their assigned date or forfeit their slot. Once you enroll as a freshman, BulSU grants honorable dismissal only after the semester starts.

### Reserve your slot on campus

Pack these 7 mandatory documents inside a **long brown envelope** labeled with your pooling number, full name, course, and campus:

- Admission email confirmation (printed and signed)
- Medical and Dental Health Assessment Form and Chest X-Ray Referral Form (completed)
- Chest X-ray result (official result taken within 6 months, plus photocopy)
- 1 white-background 2" x 2" ID photo
- Certificate of Good Moral Character (original copy)
- Grade 12 Report Card (SF9, original copy plus photocopy)
- PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)

Include a medical clearance or PWD ID only if applicable.

On your reservation date at Main Campus, complete these 3 steps in order:

1. Present your printed admission email at the University Admissions Office (temporarily at the 2nd Floor of the BulSU E-Library) for verification against the Master List of Qualifiers and an official stamp.
2. Complete your medical and dental exam at University Health Services (temporarily at Valencia Hall), submitting your emailed Health Assessment Form, external Chest X-ray result, and any medical clearance or PWD ID.
3. Submit your labeled long brown envelope and signed Medical and Dental Clearance Slip to the University Registrar Office at Valencia Hall.

Satellite campuses follow the same 3-office sequence across their local buildings. Some, including Sarmiento and Meneses, also require an online Health Information Google Form.

### Enroll online and attend orientation

After the Registrar validates your hard copies, wait for your emailed student portal credentials and enroll online on [ALAB SMS](https://alabsms.bulsu.edu.ph/). For A.Y. 2026-2027, qualified freshman hard-copy submission and enrollment ran May 13 to 23, 2026, and classes started June 29, 2026.

For A.Y. 2026-2027 orientation, the BulSU University Calendar listed **July 18, 2026**, while the Main Campus Office of Student Affairs held *Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso* earlier on **June 22 to 26, 2026** at Valencia Hall. Watch your campus and college Facebook pages for your batch's schedule.

## Portal issues and official contacts

### Fix common portal issues

- **Missing test permit or schedule email:** Check your spam folder and make sure your inbox storage is not full. If the email still does not arrive, visit your target campus's Admissions Office during the catch-up window with 1 valid ID and your application copy.
- **Missing QR code or blurred permit:** Examinees in 2025 hit a Google Chrome rendering bug where QR codes disappeared on printed permits. Try another browser or download the PDF directly before printing, and contact UAO if the code still does not show.
- **Editing submitted forms:** The portal does not allow edits after submission or stamping. Email UAO or appeal in person if you need a correction.
- **Missing program in portal dropdown:** Programs filter by selected campus. BS Psychology appears only when Main or San Rafael Campus is selected, and BS Nursing is no longer listed under Main Campus for A.Y. 2027-2028.

### Contact UAO and ICTO Helpdesk

- **University Admissions Office (Main Campus):** Beside the College of Law Building, Brgy. Guinhawa, Malolos. Email **admissions@bulsu.edu.ph**, call (044) 919-7800 loc. 1087, or message the [BulSU Admissions Office Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice).
- **ICTO Helpdesk (portal and ALAB SMS bugs):** Submit a ticket on the [BulSU ICTO Helpdesk](https://icto-helpdesk.bulsu.edu.ph/) under Information System Support. No account is needed; attach PNG, JPG, or PDF screenshots up to 10 MB and keep your ticket number. You can also email **icto@bulsu.edu.ph** or visit the ICTO office at Natividad Hall on Main Campus.
