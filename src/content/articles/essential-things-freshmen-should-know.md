Incoming freshmen and transfer students must complete orientation, medical clearance, student portal access, and gate ID compliance during their first term at Bulacan State University.

## Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso orientation

All incoming freshmen and transferees must attend *Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso*, BulSU's annual orientation before 1st semester classes open. Upperclassmen attend a shorter re-orientation each first semester.

For timing reference, the 2026 Main Campus orientation ran by college at Valencia Hall in late June (morning sessions 8:00 AM to 12:00 NN, afternoon sessions 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM). Watch your campus Student Affairs Facebook page for your college's assigned half-day session.

## Student portal (ALAB SMS) and ICTO Helpdesk

BulSU uses the **ALAB SMS Portal** for viewing grades, checking enrollment status, and submitting add, change, or drop requests (the older HEIMS Portal went under system maintenance in 2026).

Common ALAB SMS issues:

- **Cannot view grades:** Complete faculty evaluations for all instructors for the current semester. If grades remain locked after completing every evaluation, submit an ICTO helpdesk ticket.
- **Enrollment on hold for outstanding balance:** Settle unpaid balances with the Accounting Office and wait for payment status to update in ALAB SMS. If you believe the balance shown is wrong, submit an ICTO ticket or verify it in person at the Accounting Office.
- **Login problems:** Try the Forgot Password link on the login page first, then submit an ICTO helpdesk ticket if you still cannot sign in.

### How to submit an ICTO Helpdesk ticket

You do not need an account to file a ticket. Official posts cite two slightly different helpdesk URLs (`ict-helpdesk.bulsu.edu.ph` and [the ICTO Helpdesk portal](https://icto-helpdesk.bulsu.edu.ph/)); check which loads live before submitting.

1. Open the helpdesk site and choose the right unit: Information System Support (for portal, Google, or Microsoft accounts and passwords) or IT Infrastructure Assistance (for Wi-Fi, LAN, computers, or equipment).
2. Enter your full name as listed in university records, student number, college, and valid email (birthdate is required only for Google or Microsoft account requests).
3. Describe the issue and attach an optional screenshot (PNG, JPG, or PDF up to 10 MB), then submit and save your ticket number.
4. Track updates using your ticket number. If you lose your ticket number, email `icto@bulsu.edu.ph`.

## Gate ID rules and weekly uniform days

Campus guards enforce a strict **No ID, No Entry** rule. Wear your university ID visibly at all times. If you leave it at home, present your Certificate of Registration (COR) at the gate so the guard can write a remark, date it, and sign the back for temporary entry.

Recent college guides for A.Y. 2026-2027 follow this weekly schedule:

- Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays: prescribed daily uniform with black shoes (Friday can also be daily uniform if not wearing an org shirt)
- Fridays: recognized student organization or college shirt (or daily uniform)
- Wednesdays, Saturdays, and Sundays: Wash Day / Free Day (decent civilian attire)
- PATHFIT, PE, NSTP, and lab classes: wear activity uniforms or lab outfits only during those class hours

Check your college Local Student Council page for exact college pin or uniform details, and see [BulSU Dress Code, Grooming, and ID Policy](/articles/bulsu-dress-code-and-id-policy) for hair and jewelry rules.

## First-year medical and guidance requirements

- **Medical and dental exam:** Freshmen, transferees, and shifters must complete an annual physical, medical, and dental examination at University Health Services. During slot reservation, qualifiers bring their emailed Medical and Dental Assessment Form, a chest X-ray taken within 6 months, and medical clearance or PWD ID if applicable.
- **Guidance testing:** Freshmen take the standardized Mental Ability Test (MAT) through the Guidance and Counseling Services Center, followed by the College Adjustment Scale (CAS) in 2nd year and the OMNI Personality Inventory in 3rd year. Wait for your college or campus schedule.

## Student classifications on your COR

Your Certificate of Registration and class lists label your enrollment status:

- Regular student: carries the full semester load set by your curriculum.
- Irregular student: enrolled for credit with less than the full semester load.
- Working student: holds a part-time job and may carry a minimum of 15 academic units per semester (eligible to request residency extensions if approved).
- Special student: enrolled without academic credit (up to 9 units per semester for at most 2 academic years, graded Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory).

Freshmen may also join recognized student organizations voluntarily starting in their first year.
