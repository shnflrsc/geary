The Office of Student Publications and Information (OSPI) oversees campus journalism at BulSU under the Campus Journalism Act of 1991 (Republic Act 7079). Editorial board seats for both the university flagship paper and local college publications are filled through competitive written exams and panel interviews.

## The Pacesetter and college publications

*The Pacesetter* is BulSU's official university-wide student publication, distributed across the Main Campus and external campuses and funded through the student paper trust fund. Follow [The Pacesetter on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUPacesetter) or email **newsroom.pacesetter@gmail.com** for open calls and coverage inquiries.

OSPI also recognizes local student papers across colleges and satellite campuses and coordinates press training workshops. It safeguards editorial independence and handles formal complaints involving campus publications separately from regular college Student Discipline Committees.

## Editorial board positions

*The Pacesetter* Editorial Board has 14 leadership and management positions:

- Top leadership: Editor-in-Chief, Associate Editor, Managing Editor for Administration, and Managing Editor for Finance
- Section editors: News, Features, Sports, Literary, and Development Communication (DevComm)
- Creative and broadcast leads: Art Director, Online Content Manager, Circulations Manager, Photojournalism Coordinator, and Broadcast Director

## Qualifications to apply

You must meet all 5 requirements before filing an editorial board application:

- Official enrollment in **at least 15 academic units**, certified by your unit head or the University Registrar
- **At least 1 year of residence** at BulSU
- Good academic standing with no failing (5.00), incomplete (INC), or conditional (4.00) grade in any subject
- Certificate of Good Moral Character from the Dean of Student Affairs and Services, with no record of disciplinary action
- No major position in any student organization and no affiliation with any student political party

Prior campus publication experience is preferred.

## Apply and complete selection

Applications for the incoming academic year typically open between **March and April** on the BulSU Student Publications Office and *The Pacesetter* Facebook pages. Follow-up calls go out if specific section editor seats remain open.

1. Take the written exam and panel interview. An ad hoc selection committee conducts the screening: the Head of Student Publications and Information, 1 faculty member, 1 jointly accepted media practitioner, and 2 past editors chosen by the outgoing board. Recent screenings have been held at Carpio Hall and the Office of Student Publications.
2. Receive formal appointment. The selection committee endorses qualifiers to the University President for designation.
3. Serve a 1-year term. Each board serves until the close of the academic year, and the next board must be selected **at least 30 days before** the academic year ends.

## Grounds for removal

After due process, board members or staff may be removed for:

- Incompetence or breaking university rules
- Unauthorized outside affiliation
- Misuse of publication funds or property
- Defamation, invasion of privacy, or immorality
- Mocking athletes or persons with disabilities

## Campus journalism contests and awards

BulSU student journalists train and compete in local, regional, and national press conferences:

- *Pandayan Plaridel* and *Gawad Galing Plaridel*
- Regional Tertiary Press Congress
- Regional Higher Education Press Conference (RHEPC)
- Luzon-wide Higher Education Press Conference
- Annual National CONFAB

Outstanding campus writers are also eligible for Journalist of the Year at the annual Gold Gear Awards.
