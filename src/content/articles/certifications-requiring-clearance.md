## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment and documentary stamp)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Accomplished Clearance Form signed by your College Dean's Office, E-Library, Accounting Office, OSAS, and Registrar
- Official Receipt for PHP 25 per copy
- Photocopy of your TOR (if requesting for BulSU Law School)
- 1 documentary stamp (for English Proficiency certifications)
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Have your Clearance Form signed by the 5 required offices.
2. Present your valid ID and signed Clearance Form at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
3. Pay PHP 25 per copy at the Cashier's Office, keep the Official Receipt, and buy a documentary stamp if requesting an English Proficiency certification.
4. Submit your Official Receipt, signed Clearance Form, and supporting documents at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
5. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub and valid ID, then sign the release logbook.

## Covered certifications

- Units Earned
- Complete Academic Requirements (CAR)
- Undergraduate GWA
- Undergraduate English Proficiency
- Law School Eligibility

## Fee and processing time

Each certification costs PHP 25 per copy and takes up to 4 working days to process.
