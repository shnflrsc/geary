If you want to represent BulSU in regional athletics, music, dance, literary arts, or academic contests, two university offices coordinate varsity and cultural teams: the **Sports Development Office (SDO)** and the **Office of Culture and Arts (OCA)**.

## Joining BulSU varsity teams

Follow [BulSU Sports Development Office on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bulsusdo) and individual Gold Gears team pages for open tryout schedules (held at venues like the BulSU Activity Center, Bulacan Sports Complex, or partner sports courts). Students enter varsity rosters through two paths:

1. **External athlete recruitment (incoming students):** Open to incoming freshmen or transferees who won at least a **bronze medal** in Palarong Pambansa or an equivalent national meet, a **silver medal** in a regional meet, or a **gold medal** in a provincial meet. (Division and district placements do not qualify for external recruitment.)
2. **Campus tryouts and scouting:** Enrolled students can try out during open team screenings or get scouted during University Intramurals. Individual teams may set year-level or experience requirements and required gear (for example, BULPRISA/City Meet background or personal training equipment).

Coaches score athlete candidates across five weighted criteria: **Attitude (30%)**, **Level of competition (20%)**, **Sports skills (20%)**, **Academic grades (20%)**, and **Age (10%)**.

Varsity athletes train year-round and represent BulSU in meets such as the **SCUAA III Regional Olympics** (where BulSU topped the 2026 official medal tally with 121 golds and 262 total medals) and **UCLAA**. Qualified athletes receive **50% to 100% tuition scholarships** (full scholars cannot fail more than 10% of enrolled units) and may apply to stay in the **Athletes' Dormitory** upon coach and Sports Development approval. Joining outside community games requires a medical permit, parental consent, and Sports Development clearance.

## Cultural and performing arts groups

The [BulSU Office of Culture and Arts](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUOCA) recruits student performers into recognized university groups through annual auditions and campus showcases such as **SALIKSIK** (covering vocal competitions, literary arts like essay, storytelling, monologue, and oratory, instrumental solo, and visual arts including painting, rendering, and photography).

Recognized performing groups include **Hyper-Dynamics**, the university **Band**, **Lahing Kayumanggi Dance Troupe**, **Saring Himig Chorale**, and **Euphonistas**, alongside college-level cultural clubs.

- **Scholarships and support:** Qualified members may receive **50% to 100% tuition discounts** on their adviser's recommendation (confirm current monthly allowance terms with OSFAS or OCA).
- **Academic standing rule:** Full cultural scholars receive a warning at **1 failing mark** (must pass the next semester) and lose the grant at **3 failing marks**.
- **Hair-color exemption:** Cultural performers are exempted from BulSU's natural hair-color rule when required by an official competition or stage show.

## Off-campus competition clearances and cash incentives

Before any BulSU student or team leaves campus for official contests, seminars, or guestings, file the **Compliance Checklist** with the Office of Student Development, secure recommending approval from the OSAS Dean, and obtain the **University President's** signature (off-campus group travel also follows the 1:20 faculty companion ratio). Official representation counts as an excused class absence up to the 30% semester cap.

Baseline regional and national cash incentives for BulSU winners (plus 50% for coaches) are listed below. Confirm current payout amounts with SDO or OCA, as university rates may be updated:

| Competition category | Level | 1st place | 2nd place | 3rd place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Academic, Sports, and Cultural | Regional | PHP 1,000 | PHP 700 | PHP 500 |
| Academic, Sports, and Cultural | National | PHP 1,500 | PHP 1,200 | PHP 800 |
| Pageants / Beauty Contests | Regional | PHP 5,000 | PHP 3,000 | PHP 1,000 |
| Pageants / Beauty Contests | National | PHP 10,000 | PHP 8,000 | PHP 5,000 |

Top student athletes, artists, and competition winners may also qualify for annual **Gold Gear Awards** covered in [Can I Graduate With Honors?](/articles/can-i-graduate-with-honors).
