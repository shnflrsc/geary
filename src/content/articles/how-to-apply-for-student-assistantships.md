The BulSU Placement and Public Employment Service Office (PPESO) manages paid on-campus work through the Institutional Student Assistant (ISA) program at the Placement Office on the **Ground Floor of Roxas Hall**.

For opening dates and slot caps, watch the [BulSU-PPESO Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bulsu.ppeso) or email **bulsu.ppeso@bulsu.edu.ph**.

## Eligibility for student assistantships

ISA slots are limited and competitive. Make sure you meet these conditions before lining up:

- Have no failing grade (5.00) and no incomplete mark (INC) from the previous semester
- Have at least **4 hours of vacant time per day** (strongly encouraged)
- Apply starting in your **2nd semester** if you are a 1st-year student so you already have a BulSU Certificate of Grades (COG) on record
- Pass the screening set by the BulSU office requesting an assistant

## Documents to prepare

### New ISA applicants

Prepare these mandatory documents:

- Accomplished ISA Application Form, picked up at the Placement Office during the announced distribution window
- Certificate of Registration (COR) for the current semester
- Certificate of Grades (COG) from the previous semester
- Updated Class Schedule showing your daily vacant hours

If your assigned office asks for it during screening, also prepare a signed parental consent form.

### Returning ISAs

Returning ISAs only need a Recommendation Letter for reappointment from their assigned office.

## Apply for an ISA slot

1. Watch the [BulSU-PPESO Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bulsu.ppeso) for the opening post right before or at the start of each semester. Windows are very short: some semesters distribute forms for **1 day only** and cap intake early (the January 2026 intake capped applications at **60 applicants**).
2. Pick up your ISA Application Form at the Placement Office on the Ground Floor of Roxas Hall on the announced distribution day.
3. Return your filled-out form, COR, COG, and updated class schedule to the Placement Office within the **2-to-4-day filing window**.
4. Clear office assignment screening and attend the mandatory **ISA Orientation and Work Ethics** session before starting duty.

PPESO does not publish exact hourly or monthly allowance rates on its Facebook announcements. Ask the Placement Office directly about the current student assistant rate and payout schedule when you apply.

## Duty rules and academic load

Student assistants follow a **"No class, no duty"** rule and only render duty when classes are running. Never miss a regular lecture or lab to cover office hours, or you risk absences and drops under [University Attendance Policies](/articles/university-attendance-policies).

Under university academic load rules, working students may carry a reduced minimum load of **15 academic units** per semester.

## Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES)

The Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) places financially deserving students in temporary jobs during summer and Christmas breaks.

BulSU PPESO has not recently posted a campus-hosted SPES call, focusing instead on ISA hiring, campus job fairs, labor education seminars, and DSWD cash-for-work announcements. Monitor your city or municipal PESO office Facebook page before vacation periods if you want to apply for SPES.
