BulSU enforces student discipline progressively, starting from verbal counsel and constructive **Transformative Experience** hours up to suspension, dismissal, or permanent expulsion. Alongside the Student Handbook, the university also circulates *Enhanced Guidelines on Student Conduct, Discipline, and Grievance Intervention* through OSAS and campus Student Welfare offices, so confirm current sanction updates with your campus OSAS office if you are involved in a case.

## Categories of offenses and sanctions

Penalties escalate with repeat offenses. Committing three different violations triggers a 2nd-offense penalty based on your most serious infraction; four different violations trigger a 3rd-offense penalty.

| Tier | Common examples | Typical progressive sanctions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Littering, unauthorized posters or flyers, vandalism, class disruption, unauthorized selling or solicitation | Verbal reprimand, then written reprimand plus 4 hours Transformative Experience, then 10 hours plus Guidance Intervention (with restitution or cleaning). A 4th offense carries a 2-week suspension. |
| Less Grave | Smoking, gambling, or alcohol on campus; malicious accusations; fraud or impersonation; disrespecting officials; entering or damaging closed premises | Written reprimand plus 4 to 8 hours Transformative Experience and parent dialogue with the Dean, escalating to 10 to 32 hours plus Guidance Intervention (with restitution). A 4th offense carries a 2-week suspension. |
| Grave | Theft, lewd conduct on campus, physical, verbal, or sexual abuse, cyberbullying, hazing, threats, illegal drugs, carrying weapons | Written reprimand or 1-week to 1-semester suspension, escalating to dismissal or immediate expulsion (plus police turnover for drugs, weapons, or serious injury). |
| Academic Dishonesty | Cheating on exams, plagiarism in theses or papers, falsifying or forging academic records or official documents | Cheating: zero score (1st), failing grade plus 16 hours (2nd), 1-month suspension plus failing grade (3rd). Plagiarism: failed output, then failed subject plus 2-week suspension, then 1-semester suspension. Forgery: 1-semester suspension, then 1-year suspension, then dismissal. |

Transformative Experience hours are scheduled during your vacant periods so they do not interrupt regular classes. The university may also withhold academic records or a degree while a disciplinary case is still pending. Note that student leaders and Student Government officers are also subject to Board of Student Discipline proceedings for official misconduct, such as altering or falsifying project liquidation documents.

**Checking plagiarism and AI similarity before you submit:** If you are preparing a thesis or major academic output and want guidance on plagiarism or AI checking, visit the **4th or 5th floor of the BulSU e-Library** to inquire with the assigned library staff (see [BulSU Library Guide](/articles/bulsu-library-guide) for full plagiarism-check steps).

## Formal hearing process and appeal timelines

Any person may file a sworn written complaint with witness statements and supporting evidence before the college or campus **Student Discipline Committee** (chaired by the College Secretary or campus equivalent, alongside faculty, Student Governor, PTA, arbiter, and secretary; campus press cases are handled through Student Publications).

1. **5 working days to answer:** If the committee finds a *prima facie* case, it sends you the complaint and evidence. You have 5 working days to submit a sworn written answer with supporting documents.
2. **Closed, non-litigious hearing:** Held within 5 working days of your answer to clarify facts. You may bring legal counsel at your own expense and submit questions through the committee. Failing to appear after proper notice allows the hearing to proceed without you.
3. **Committee recommendation and Board decision:** A majority recommendation goes to the **Board of Student Discipline** within 5 working days, and the Board issues its decision within 5 working days.
4. **10 working days for Motion for Reconsideration:** Either party has 10 working days from receipt of the decision to file a Motion for Reconsideration with the Board.
5. **Final appeal for dismissal or expulsion:** If your motion is denied and the sanction is **dismissal** (at least 1 year exclusion, signed by the President) or **expulsion** (permanent severance recorded on your TOR), you have **10 working days** to appeal to the **University President or Board of Regents**.

**Summary proceedings** skip formal hearings when a respondent admits guilt in writing, is caught in the act by university authorities, faces overwhelming evidence, or is a habitual offender punished three or more times before.

## Can a suspension record be expunged later?

Suspensions lasting at least one semester are recorded in your permanent file. After **3 years** from the date of the finding, you may petition the **University President** (or designee) to expunge the record based on the severity of the violation, your overall standing, and proof of good conduct since. Once approved, the suspension notation is removed from your Registrar file.

If you need to report unfair treatment, grade issues, or harassment directed at you, read [How to File a Student Complaint](/articles/how-to-file-a-student-complaint).
