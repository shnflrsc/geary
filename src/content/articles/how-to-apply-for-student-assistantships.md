If you want paid on-campus work that fits around your classes, apply for BulSU's Institutional Student Assistant (ISA) program. Applications are managed by the BulSU Placement and Public Employment Service Office (PPESO) at the Placement Office on the **Ground Floor of Roxas Hall**.

For opening dates and slot caps, watch the [BulSU-PPESO Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bulsu.ppeso) or email `bulsu.ppeso@bulsu.edu.ph`.

## Who can apply

ISA slots are limited and competitive. Make sure you meet these conditions before lining up:

- **Clean previous semester:** No failing grade (`5.00`) and no incomplete mark (`INC`) from the prior semester.
- **Daily vacant time:** Applicants with at least **4 hours of vacant time per day** are strongly encouraged.
- **Year level:** First-year students are encouraged to wait and apply starting their **Second Semester** so they already have a BulSU Certificate of Grades (COG) on record.
- **Office screening:** You must pass the screening set by the specific BulSU office requesting an assistant.

## What to prepare

Requirements depend on whether you are applying for the first time or renewing your appointment.

### New ISA applicants

- Accomplished ISA Application Form, picked up at the Placement Office during the announced distribution window
- Certificate of Registration (COR) for the current semester
- Certificate of Grades (COG) from the previous semester
- Updated Class Schedule showing your daily vacant hours
- Signed parental consent form, if requested during office screening

### Returning ISAs

- Recommendation Letter for reappointment from your assigned office

## Application steps

1. **Watch for the PPESO opening post.** Calls open right before or at the start of each semester, and windows are very short. Some semesters distribute forms for a single day only and cap intake early. For example, the January 2026 intake capped applications at **60 applicants**.
2. **Pick up your Application Form at Roxas Hall.** Go to the Placement Office on the Ground Floor of Roxas Hall on the announced distribution day.
3. **Submit your complete documents before the deadline.** Return your filled-out form, COR, COG, and updated class schedule to the Placement Office within the 2-to-4-day filing window.
4. **Clear office screening and attend orientation.** Accepted applicants complete office assignment screening and attend the mandatory **ISA Orientation and Work Ethics** session before starting duty.

PPESO does not publish exact hourly or monthly allowance rates on its Facebook announcements. Ask the Placement Office directly about the current student assistant rate and payout schedule when you apply.

## Duty rules and academic load

- **"No class, no duty" guideline:** Student assistants only render duty when classes are running. Never miss a regular lecture or lab to cover office hours, or you risk absences and drops under [University Attendance Policies](/articles/university-attendance-policies).
- **Working-student unit floor:** Under university academic load rules, working students may carry a reduced minimum load of **15 academic units** per semester.

## What about SPES during school breaks?

The Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) places financially deserving students in temporary jobs during summer and Christmas breaks.

BulSU PPESO has not recently posted a campus-hosted SPES call, focusing instead on ISA hiring, campus job fairs, labor education seminars, and DSWD cash-for-work announcements. Monitor your city or municipal PESO office Facebook page before vacation periods if you want to apply for SPES.
