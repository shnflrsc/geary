## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Accomplished Clearance Form signed by your College Dean's Office, E-Library, Accounting Office, OSAS, and Registrar (for the first copy only)
- Official Receipt for PHP 25 per copy
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Complete the 5-office Clearance Form first if this is your first copy.
2. Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
3. Pay PHP 25 per copy at the Cashier's Office and keep the Official Receipt.
4. Submit your Official Receipt and Clearance Form (if required) at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
5. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub and valid ID, then sign the release logbook.

## Fee and processing time

A Certificate of Grades costs PHP 25 per copy. Processing takes up to 4 working days for records from 2008 to present, or 11 working days for records from 2007 and earlier.
