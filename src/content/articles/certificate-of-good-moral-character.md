## How to get

**Location**
- Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) (Main Campus or your external campus OSAS office)
- Cashier's Office (for payment)

**Requirements**
- Accomplished Good Moral Character request form (provided at the OSAS counter)
- No active or pending disciplinary case on record
- Official Receipt for PHP 25 per copy

**Step-by-step**
1. Fill out the Good Moral Character request form at the OSAS counter so staff can check your name against the university disciplinary database.
2. Take the billing form from OSAS to the Cashier's Office and pay PHP 25 per copy.
3. Return to OSAS with your Official Receipt and wait for the Dean's signature to claim your certificate.

## Fee and processing time

The certificate costs PHP 25 per copy. Walk-in release normally takes about 5 minutes once you present your Official Receipt at OSAS.
