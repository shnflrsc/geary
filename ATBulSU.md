# ATBulSU Master Research Document (A.Y. 2025-2026 & A.Y. 2026-2027)

Synthesized reference document for the Admission Test for Bulacan State University (ATBulSU), compiled from general web/official PDFs, official Facebook announcements, and student-reported accounts (2024-2026). Main Campus (Malolos) is the default; satellite campuses (Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, San Rafael, Sarmiento) are labeled where they differ. Circular citations (such as Geary itself) and contradicted third-party blog claims are excluded or flagged in Section 9.

---

## 1. Application & Registration

### Timelines Across Cycles
- **A.Y. 2025-2026:** Online applications opened **November 7, 2024** (student press) and officially closed **January 12, 2025** (BulSU Admissions Office). Applicants booked an in-person hard-copy submission date within 2 weeks of online filing (Main Campus hours: weekdays 8:00 AM-7:00 PM; Saturdays 8:00 AM-5:00 PM).
- **A.Y. 2026-2027:** Moved two months earlier under BOR Resolution No. 213, s. 2025. Online applications ran **September 22 to December 19, 2025**; hard-copy submission ran **September 29 to December 20, 2025** (Mon-Sat, 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, excluding Sundays/holidays). Rescheduled December dates for external campuses (Dec 8, 2025 advisory): Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses moved from Dec 13 to Dec 17, 2025; San Rafael and Sarmiento moved from Dec 20 to Dec 18, 2025.

### Eligibility & Separate Tracks (Official)
- **Freshman Eligibility:** Open to Senior High School (SHS) candidates for graduation, SHS graduates from public/DepEd-recognized private schools, SHS graduates of accredited secondary schools abroad, and **Alternative Learning System (ALS)** graduates holding Accreditation & Equivalency (A&E) certification. Applicants **must not have enrolled in or attended any college or university** prior to the applied academic year.
- **Shifters, Transferees & Second Coursers (Barred from Freshman Track):** Must apply through a separate UAO track (processing fee: `NONE`). Covers Main Campus shifters (incoming 2nd/3rd year), External Campus shifters (**incoming 3rd-year only**), transferees (incoming 2nd year), and second coursers. Applying through Freshman Admission leads to **disqualification / blacklisting across all BulSU programs**. Separate exams run around July (e.g., Bustos Campus held its transferee/shifter exam on July 31, 2025; Main Campus shifter/transferee online enrollment ran June 15-20, 2026).
- **Other Separate Portal Tracks:** `admissions.bulsu.edu.ph` hosts separate forms for GSAT (Graduate), ETEEAP (working professionals with 5+ years relevant experience), Night Class (Main Campus only: BBA Marketing Mgmt & BSIT), CPE, and CPTE.

### Step-by-Step Registration Workflow & Test Permits
1. **Online Form:** Access the canonical UAO admissions portal (`https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/`) and complete the 7-step Freshmen Form (Personal Information including Priority/Family/Additional Circumstances, Educational Background, Application Details, Family Background, Medical Records, Medical History, Supporting Documents) and upload scanned files.
2. **On-Site Hard-Copy Validation:** Report on your chosen appointment date to your target campus's Admissions Office (Main Campus: beside College of Law Bldg., Brgy. Guinhawa, Malolos, or temporarily 2nd Floor University E-Library during peak periods; ~5-minute validation). Satellite campuses (Bustos, Meneses, Sarmiento, etc.) run campus-level validation.
3. **Test Permit Issuance:** Qualified applicants receive an emailed **ATBulSU Test Permit** (date, time, testing center, room, and QR code). In A.Y. 2025-2026, permit emails began **February 6, 2025**; in A.Y. 2026-2027, Meneses emailed Jan 26-31, 2026 and general permits went out around **February 1, 2026**. Print on **short bond paper** (Sarmiento 2025 advisory specified 2 copies: 1 inside clear envelope, 1 to present to proctor).

### Fee & Disqualification Rules
- **Testing Fee:** **None (free)** — no testing or application fee is charged for ATBulSU (confirmed per Citizen's Charter 2025 1st Ed. and BulSU Admissions Office; ignore the PHP 300.00 entry in Citizen's Charter 2025 2nd Ed.).
- **Official Disqualifiers (BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025):** Misrepresentation in any form or portal, violation of instructions, non-submission of hard copies on schedule, or applying as a freshman despite prior college attendance.
- **Student-Reported Mistakes:** Accidentally checking unverified Affirmative Action categories (e.g., First-Gen) or attempting to alter stamped course/campus choices on exam day (risks blacklisting).

## 2. Requirements & Documentation

### Online Upload & On-Site Hard-Copy Verification (Official)
- **File Formats & Photo Specs:** `.jpeg` for the **2" x 2" ID photo** (white background with printed name tag; no computer-generated/composite photos) and `.pdf` for the Grade 11 Report Card. *No maximum MB/KB file-size limit is published for the admissions portal.*
- **Core Hard-Copy Documents:**
  - Certified True Copy (with school seal) of **Grade 11 Report Card (SF9 / Form 138)** (1st and 2nd semesters). *Form 137 (SF10) and Certification of Enrollment are not required in 2025-2026 freshman checklists.*
  - **2 originally signed copies of the Declaration Form** (from portal), each with a hard-copy 2" x 2" white-background photo with name tag.
  - Certified True Copy / photocopy of **Proof of Residency (Barangay Certificate)**.
  - Photocopy of **latest Proof of Billing** (electric, water, internet bill, or ITR; Barangay Certification if unavailable).

### Optional Special Category / Affirmative Action Documents (Official)
- **Priority Demographic Group (max 1 counted, 3 pts):** ICC/IP (NCIP/tribal certification), 4Ps (ID or Barangay cert), PWD / Students with Special Needs (SWD/Barangay PWD ID), or ALS Graduate (A&E Assessment & Certification).
- **Family & Social Support Group (max 1 counted, 3 pts):** Solo Parent / Child of Solo Parent (CSWDO/Municipal Hall ID or cert), Orphan living with relatives (Death Certificate/s of parent/s), Orphaned/Abandoned in orphanage (orphanage certification), or First-Generation College Student (**Discrepancy — note in article & advise applicants to confirm with UAO:** BOR Reso. No. 213 specifies *Affidavit of Non-Graduation*, while the Sep 17, 2025 Admissions post specifies *CSWDO/MSWDO certification*).
- **Additional Categories:** GIDA resident (Barangay certification, 3 pts), Exemplary Artistic/Athletic Ability (School Head or civic/religious org head certificate, 3 pts), BulSU Adopted Community / Child of BulSU Employee (Extension Office cert or parent's Employee ID, 5 pts), Working Student (Certificate of Employment, per Sep 17, 2025 list), and TVL NC I / NC II (preferred for CIT).

### Exam-Day vs. Slot Reservation Physical Packets
- **Exam Day:** Short transparent plastic envelope containing: printed Test Permit with QR code (short bond paper), valid photo ID, **2 black ballpoint pens (no gel or rollerball)**, small alcohol in transparent container (plus transparent water bottle only).
- **Slot Reservation Day (Long Brown Envelope):** Printed reservation/congratulations email (signed), completed Medical & Dental Health Assessment Form + Chest X-Ray Referral Form, recent **official Chest X-ray result (within 6 months)** + photocopy, 1 pc 2" x 2" white-background photo, **Original Certificate of Good Moral Character**, **Original Grade 12 Report Card (SF9)** + photocopy, **PSA Birth Certificate photocopy**, and Medical Clearance / PWD ID if applicable.

## 3. Examination Schedule & Venue

### Exam Dates, Volume & Batches
- **Format:** Single-day, two-batch (morning and afternoon), paper-based exam administered **simultaneously** across Malolos Main Campus and all 5 satellite campuses (Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses/Bulakan, San Rafael, Sarmiento/CSJDM).
- **A.Y. 2025-2026:** **Sunday, March 16, 2025** — officially **29,695** freshmen applicants across 6 campuses. Morning batch: **8:15 AM-10:15 AM**; Afternoon batch: **1:15 PM-3:15 PM** (*The Communiqué*, official student publication of BulSU College of Arts and Letters).
- **A.Y. 2026-2027:** **Sunday, March 1, 2026** — ~**22,000** at Main Campus and **>9,000** across external campuses (~**31,000** total, Admissions advisory). Use *The Communiqué* batch windows (**8:15 AM-10:15 AM** / **1:15 PM-3:15 PM**) as the reference exam schedule; external press (*Pilipino Star Ngayon*) noted queues and staging starting around 6:00 AM-7:30 AM.
- **Strict Scheduling Rules:** Arrive at least **1 hour early** (no exact late-arrival minute cutoff published). Testing center assignment is computer-generated and non-transferable; morning takers cannot switch to afternoon and vice versa; no rescheduling for conflicts with other CETs. Passers still enroll at their chosen campus even if assigned to a different testing center.

### Gate Entry, Traffic, Dress Code & Prohibited Items
- **Dress Code (Official):** **White shirt, polo, or blouse** (colored tops prohibited in 2026 advisory).
- **Campus Entry & Traffic:** Examinees only inside campus (no parents, guardians, or chaperones, except verified PWD companions). For ATBulSU 2026 (Malolos): Main Campus Gates 1-4 served as entry/exit; road from Provincial Agriculture Office to Philippine Red Cross Bulacan Chapter closed 5:00 AM-6:00 PM; Mojon-Guinhawa Diversion Road one-way; drop-offs at former Caltex Gas Station and Graceland Mall frontage. External campuses: foot entry only.
- **Strictly Prohibited (Official — treated as cheating if found after exam starts):** All electronic devices (cellphones, calculators, cameras, e-cigarettes, iPods, iPads, AirPods even if off), **watches and fitness trackers of any kind**, jewelry/accessories, headwear covering ears (religious headwear e.g. hijab exempted), headsets/headphones, **all bags**, food/candies, wallets/valuables (keep needed money inside transparent envelope), and outside paper.
- **Security & Baggage Issues:** Pocket/mask checks enforced; eyes and ears must remain visible. BulSU provides **no baggage storage** and disclaims liability. In 2025 and 2026, student publications (*The Communiqué*, *The Al-Manar*) documented 2-hour traffic approaching Gate 1, piles of unattended bags, PHP 50/bag external storage fees, missing items, and lack of shade for waiting parents.
- **PWD Accommodation:** Email `admissions@bulsu.edu.ph` with subject `PWD-ASSISTANCE` (e.g., `PWD-ASSISTANCE 2025`) describing needed assistance and attaching PWD ID/certification.

## 4. Examination Coverage & Review

### Official Parameters
- **Paper-based OMR exam** (~2 hours total, confirmed by procurement RFQs for printed questionnaires/answer sheets and 2-hour batch windows).
- **Calculators strictly prohibited** (manual solving required). Use black ballpoint pens only (not gel/rollerball).
- **Scratch Paper:** **Outside scratch paper is strictly banned**; BulSU provides scratch paper during the test (per Mar 11, 2025 Admissions guidelines).
- **No official syllabus, item count, per-subtest timer, or right-minus-wrong policy** is published by BulSU. Board/licensure programs officially require **80% or better** on the entrance exam (BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025).

### Student-Reported Coverage, Difficulty & Tips (2024-2026)
- **Format:** Roughly **200 multiple-choice items in 2 hours** (~36 seconds/item; one taker noted answer sheets printed up to 300 numbers despite 200 items, with a 100-item General Knowledge section at the back).
- **Subtests:** **Mathematics, Science, Language (English & Filipino), and General Knowledge**. Multiple 2024-2025 takers confirmed **no Abstract Reasoning** section appeared.
- **High-Yield Topics:**
  - *Math:* SHS STEM & ABM mix — Statistics & Probability, number-heavy Business Math (manual computation), Trigonometry, Algebra, Permutations/Combinations, Sets, Differential Calculus.
  - *Science:* Applied SHS STEM — Earth Science (rocks, plate tectonics, volcanoes), Physical Science, Biology (photosynthesis, plant anatomy), Basic Chemistry (periodic table), Basic Physics (formulas).
  - *Language (English & Filipino):* Grammar, vocabulary, parts of speech, figures of speech, reading comprehension.
- **Difficulty & Pacing Tips:** Multiple takers rated ATBulSU **harder than PUPCET** due to heavy manual Math and applied Science under tight time pressure. Passers recommend: (1) starting at your strongest section or the rear General Knowledge items so Math does not eat the clock, (2) skipping hard items immediately and returning later, and (3) answering every item rather than leaving blanks.

## 5. Results & Qualification

### Turnaround & Release Channels
- **A.Y. 2025-2026:** Released **May 16, 2025** (~61 days after Mar 16 exam; ~31.3%-31.6% passing rate per student press).
- **A.Y. 2026-2027:** Released **April 17, 2026** (47 days after Mar 1 exam) — ~**10,600 qualifiers** out of ~31,000 examinees (**34.19% passing rate**).
- **Where Checked:** Official alphabetical **PDF Lists of Qualifiers** organized by college and degree program (including BulSU Affirmative Action Program beneficiaries), posted on `bulsu.edu.ph` and announced on BulSU Official / BulSU Admissions Office Facebook pages. Non-qualifiers learn their status by absence from the posted list.

### Waitlist & Reconsideration (Official vs. Practice)
- **Official Policy:** BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025 defines **Qualifiers** and directs shortlisted applicants to college interviews/skill tests, but contains **no written waitlist or reconsideration rubric**.
- **Operational Practice (Student-Reported & May 2025 *iJEP* Study):** BulSU runs a two-stage funnel. Once initial qualifiers miss slot-reservation deadlines and forfeit seats, UAO automatically emails ranked non-qualifiers in **batches** from **May/June through July/August** (no separate recon form required).
- **Strict Recon Terms:** Reconsideration emails specify narrow or single-day reporting windows (e.g., May 20-22, 2026 first batch; "JUNE 10, 2026 ONLY" Malolos offer) where missing the date forfeits the slot, and the offered program is non-negotiable.
- **Anti-Fixer Advisory (Official, Apr 29, 2026):** BulSU warned that individuals offering to facilitate admission for failed examinees are unauthorized and prohibited; alumni on Reddit likewise report "backer" endorsement letters are not entertained.

## 6. Course Selection & Cutoffs

### Course Choices & Locking (Official)
- **Two Choices Only:** Applicants select a **1st Choice Program** (priority) and an **allied 2nd Choice Program** (3rd choice was discontinued after A.Y. 2022-2023). Unsuccessful 1st-choice applicants are automatically evaluated for their 2nd choice.
- **No Post-Submission / Post-Result Switching:** Choices lock once hard copies are stamped. Admitted freshmen must complete **1 full academic year** before applying to shift programs.

### Grade 11 Cutoffs & 100-Point Composite Formula (BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025)
- **Grade 11 Minimums:**
  - *Board / Licensure Programs:* Grade 11 **GWA >= 87%** (lowered from 90% in Jan 2024); **>= 85%** in every Grade 11 subject, EXCEPT **>= 87%** in Math & Science (Engineering, Architecture, Accountancy), **>= 87%** in Major (Secondary Education), and **>= 87%** in Science (Nursing, Psychology, Medical Technology). Plus **>= 80% ATBulSU exam score**.
  - *Non-Board Programs:* Grade 11 **GWA >= 85%**.
  - *CIT:* No strict GWA floor; TVL Track with relevant **NC I / NC II** preferred.
  - *CSER:* Sports track and provincial/regional/national athletes preferred.
- **Composite Weighting (Tables 1-3):**
  - *Standard (No Interview):* ATBulSU **50%**, Grade 11 GWA **15%**, SHS Strand **15%** (Related: 15, Allied: 10, Not Related: 5), Affirmative Action **20%**.
  - *With Interview:* ATBulSU **50%**, Grade 11 GWA **10%**, SHS Strand **10%** (Related: 10, Allied: 7, Not Related: 5), Interview **10%**, Affirmative Action **20%**.
  - *College of Engineering (Table 2, PTC-ACBET prep):* ATBulSU **40%**, **COE Exam 20%**, Grade 11 GWA **10%**, SHS Strand **10%** (STEM: 10, GAS: 5, Others: 2), Affirmative Action **20%**.
- **Affirmative Action Allocation (State bug in article — printed Subtotal: 20 pts; listed maxima sum to 19 pts):** Bulacan residency (2 pts) vs. Other Region 3 (1 pt); Priority Group [max 1: ICC/IP, 4Ps, PWD/SSN, ALS] (3 pts); Family Support [max 1: Solo Parent child, Orphan/Abandoned, First-Gen] (3 pts); GIDA (3 pts); Artistic/Athletic (3 pts); Adopted Community / Child of BulSU Employee (5 pts).
- **Additional College Screening:** UAO notifies ranked qualifiers of college-administered interviews, specialization exams (e.g., 20% COE Exam), or skill/talent tests. Nursing qualifiers on Reddit describe their post-qualification interview as largely a formality. College of Law (JD/MLS) is a separate post-baccalaureate track. Specific 2024-2026 CAFA portfolio or CSER physical test rubrics were not retrieved.
- **Campus Availability Note (BS Nursing):** **BS Nursing and the College of Nursing are no longer listed under Main Campus** in the BulSU Admissions Office's programs offered post for A.Y. 2027-2028; freshman BSN intake is restricted to **San Rafael Campus** (86 qualifiers in A.Y. 2026-2027).

- **Canonical ICTO Portals (Confirmed):** Admissions at `https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/` (Freshmen form: `/freshmen`); student enrollment/schedules at **ALAB SMS** (`https://alabsms.bulsu.edu.ph/`); technical support at **ICTO Helpdesk** (`https://icto-helpdesk.bulsu.edu.ph/`).
- **Legacy / Deprecated URLs (Do not link in article):** `bulsu.priisms.online/padmission` (early 2025) and `bulsu.heims.ph/admission` (late 2025 / still printed in Citizen's Charter 2026 1st Ed.). BulSU paused Registrar/Accounting transactions on June 24, 2025 during its SMS transition.

### Reported Technical Issues & Verified Fixes
- **Missing Permit / Schedule Email:** Check spam and ensure email storage is not full. In 2025, applicants without emails reported in person to the Admissions Office (temporarily 2nd Floor University E-Library, Malolos) during a set window (Feb 17-28, 2025) with 1 valid ID and their personal application copy.
- **Missing QR Code / Blurred Permit:** Widespread 2025 rendering bug in Google Chrome; no official automated fix published.
- **Other Student-Reported Glitches:** Testing-center mismatches, inaccessible submitted forms, "Validation Failed" errors, campus-filtered program dropdowns (e.g., BS Psychology hidden unless Main or San Rafael is selected), peak-day outages, and vanishing block sections during enrollment.
- **Post-Submission Edits:** **No self-service editing** once submitted/stamped; applicants must email `admissions@bulsu.edu.ph` or appeal in person.
- **Official Support Contacts:**
  - *University Admissions Office (UAO):* **admissions@bulsu.edu.ph**, (044) 919-7800 loc. 1087 (Main Campus, beside College of Law Bldg., Malolos), Facebook `BulSUAdmissionsOffice`.
  - *ICTO Helpdesk:* `https://icto-helpdesk.bulsu.edu.ph/` (no account needed; select **Information System Support**; attach PNG/JPG/PDF up to 10 MB; keep ticket number), email **icto@bulsu.edu.ph**, or walk in at Main Campus ICTO Office (Carpio Hall Room 103).

## 8. Enrollment & Admission Confirmation

### A.Y. 2026-2027 Official Milestones (BulSU University Calendar)
- **May 13-23, 2026:** Qualified freshman hard-copy submission (for qualifiers who filed reservation), student number issuance, and online enrollment.
- **June 15-20, 2026:** Shiftee/Transferee/Second-Degree online enrollment; **June 22-26, 2026:** Late enrollees.
- **June 29, 2026:** Start of 1st Semester classes; **June 29-July 11, 2026:** Change of Matriculation.
- **Orientation (State both dates in article):** University Calendar lists **July 18, 2026**, while Main Campus OSA's actual execution (*Handog ni BulSU kay Bunso*) ran **June 22-26, 2026** at Valencia Hall.

### Main Campus 3-Step Reservation & Enrollment Flow (Apr 30, 2026 Official Advisory)
1. **Step 1 — University Admissions Office** (temporarily BulSU E-Library 2nd Floor): Verify printed admission email against Master List of Qualifiers and receive official stamp.
2. **Step 2 — University Health Services** (temporarily Valencia Hall in 2026; 2nd Floor E-Library in 2025): Medical and dental examination is completed **during reservation day**. Bring completed Medical & Dental Assessment Form (emailed), **recent external Chest X-ray (within 6 months)**, medical clearance if applicable, and PWD ID if applicable.
3. **Step 3 — University Registrar Office** (Valencia Hall): Submit long brown envelope labeled with pooling number, name, course, and campus containing 2x2 photo, Original Good Moral Certificate, Original + photocopy Grade 12 SF9, PSA Birth Certificate photocopy, signed email confirmation, and signed Medical & Dental Clearance Slip.

### Satellite Example, Forfeiture & ALAB SMS Transition
- **Sarmiento Campus (May 18-19, 2026):** Step 1 Admissions (Bldg. B 1st Flr), Step 2 Infirmary (Bldg. C Social Hall 3rd Flr), Step 3 Registrar (Bldg. B 1st Flr). Deficiencies accepted until May 26; printed passers list with highlighted name accepted if email missing. Sarmiento & Meneses health units also required an online Health Information Google Form.
- **Forfeiture & Withdrawal:** Missing the assigned reservation date forfeits admission. Under BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025, admitted and enrolled freshmen are **only granted an honorable dismissal once the semester starts**.
- **ALAB SMS Portal:** After hard-copy validation at the Registrar, wait for emailed portal login credentials and enroll via `https://alabsms.bulsu.edu.ph/`.

## 9. Resolved Rulings & Unverified Gaps

### Developer Rulings on Source Contradictions (Final for Article)
1. **Testing Fee:** **No testing fee (free / NONE).** Disregard Citizen's Charter 2025 (2nd Ed.) PHP 300.00 listing.
2. **Canonical Admissions URL:** **`https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/`**. Do not link legacy `bulsu.heims.ph/admission` or `bulsu.priisms.online/padmission`.
3. **BS Nursing (BSN) Campus Availability:** BS Nursing and the College of Nursing are **no longer listed under Main Campus** in the BulSU Admissions Office's programs offered post for A.Y. 2027-2028 (intake is under San Rafael Campus).
4. **First-Gen Proof Document:** **Note the discrepancy in the article and advise applicants to confirm with UAO** (BOR Reso. No. 213 lists *Affidavit of Non-Graduation*; Admissions' Sep 17, 2025 post lists *CSWDO/MSWDO certification*).
5. **Exam Batch Start Times:** Follow *The Communiqué* (`8:15-10:15 AM` morning, `1:15-3:15 PM` afternoon) as the official batch windows; treat *Pilipino Star Ngayon*'s `6:00-7:30 AM` as queue/staging start.
6. **Main vs. Satellite Exam Timing:** All 6 campuses test **simultaneously** on one day. Exclude *SchoolFinderPH* (erroneously claimed satellites test a month later and misnamed San Rafael as "Pulilan").
7. **Scratch Paper Policy:** **Outside scratch paper is banned**; BulSU provides scratch paper during the test.
8. **Course Choices:** **2 choices only** (1st priority + allied 2nd, per BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025). Disregard outdated 2023-2024 "3rd choice" PDF.
9. **Affirmative Action Point Arithmetic:** **State the bug explicitly in the article** — BOR Reso. No. 213, s. 2025 prints `Subtotal: 20 pts`, while the 6 listed category maxima sum to `19 pts` (`2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 5`).
10. **Orientation Dates (A.Y. 2026-2027):** **State both** the University Calendar date (**July 18, 2026**) and the actual Main Campus execution dates (**June 22-26, 2026**).
11. **Medical/Dental Exam Timing:** Completed as **Step 2 during reservation day**.
12. **Watches:** **All watches are banned** (analog and digital/smartwatches alike).
