The BulSU e-Library on the Main Campus gives registered students free study spaces, iPads, computer stations, print and digital collections, online research help, and official Turnitin plagiarism checking.

## Hours and online portals

- **Main Campus e-Library hours:** Monday to Friday from **7:00 AM to 7:00 PM**, and Saturday from **8:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, with **no noon break**. During major exam weeks such as midterms, the e-Library occasionally opens **24-hour service** via QR reservation.
- **External campuses:** Schedules vary by campus (for example, Meneses Campus operates Monday to Thursday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM with online-only transactions on Fridays). Check your campus library page for updates.
- **Official links:** Browse the [BulSU Library OPAC](https://library.bulsu.edu.ph), check guides and forms on the [BulSU Library GSite](https://bit.ly/AklatanBulSU), or follow [BulSU e-Library on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUeLibrary) for advisories and automated transaction system reservations.

## Learning Commons and Smart Device Zone

Both tech spaces inside the Main Campus e-Library are **free** on a first-come, first-served basis for library-registered students. Just bring your **BulSU ID** and log in at the counter:

- **Learning Commons (Ground Floor):** Collaborative study area with **iPads** available for research and coursework.
- **Smart Device Zone (6th Floor):** Desktop **computer units with free internet** and access to subscribed and open online databases.
- **Time limit:** **1 hour and 30 minutes** per session, with a **30-minute extension** allowed if no one else is waiting.

## Online research help (LIRA)

If you need help locating books, journal sources, or fair-use scans of print references and theses, use **Library Information and Research Assistance (LIRA)**:

1. Submit your inquiry through the [LIRA Request Form](http://bit.ly/bulsulibrarylira).
2. A librarian checks available print, digital, and Open Educational Resources (OERs) and emails your materials or alternative suggestions, normally within **20 minutes** during operating hours.

## Turnitin plagiarism and AI checking

All library-registered Laboratory High School, undergraduate, and graduate students can request free **Turnitin plagiarism and AI checking** before submitting research papers, capstones, or theses. Processing takes **5 working days**.

Prepare your file before submitting through the [Main Campus Plagiarism Check Form](https://bit.ly/bulsuplagscan) (external campuses may use a separate campus link):

- One PDF copy containing your Title Page, author names, college/department, and Chapters 1 to 5 (or 1 to 9), **excluding** preliminary pages and appendices
- For group papers, only one member per group should submit
- For Graduate School students, an endorsement letter from the Graduate School Office

**Passing limits:** BulSU requires **10% or lower** plagiarism similarity and **35% or lower** AI score. Papers within both limits receive an emailed **Plagiarism Certificate** and **AI Use Declaration Form** (plus Confidentiality Agreement and Open Access Permission forms for theses and capstones). Papers above either limit receive the similarity report for revision before resubmission. For in-person plagiarism or AI checking questions, visit the **4th or 5th floor of the BulSU e-Library**.

## Borrowing print books and materials

| Collection | Use or borrowing limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circulation Books (Foreign and Filipiniana) | Overnight borrowing | Allowed when multiple copies exist or demand is low |
| Fiction Books | 1 week (renewable twice) | Renewals allowed unless another reader placed a hold |
| Reserve and Reference Books | Room use or photocopy only | Core references stay inside the library |
| Periodicals | Room use or photocopy only | Maximum of 3 titles at a time |
| Theses and Dissertations | Room use only | Available inside for literature reviews |
| Cartographic and Audio-Visual Materials | Room use or classroom use | Submit a written request letter at least 1 day ahead for classroom use |

Return borrowed items and settle any library fees before the end of each semester. Pending library obligations will hold your clearance for [TOR and certificate requests](/articles/how-to-request-transcript-and-certificates) or [graduation](/articles/bulsu-graduation-requirements).
