The Office of Student Organizations and Activities (OSOA) and the BulSU-OSO Executive Board accredit and monitor campus organizations. Check the [BulSU OSOA Main Campus Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/osobulsu) each academic year for the official list of Recognized Student Organizations (RSOs), upload portals, and letter templates.

## Joining organizations

Membership in recognized BulSU organizations is voluntary and open to all students, including incoming freshmen. You can join more than one organization, subject to each group's constitution and voting rules.

Recognized groups fall into seven categories: college-based and campus-based academic societies, socio-civic, environmental, lifestyle, political, multi-faith (where OSOA remains faith-neutral), and fraternities.

BulSU limits university-wide recognition to **a maximum of 3 fraternities** at any time. Any fraternity that violates university rules or the Anti-Hazing Law (RA 8049) faces automatic revocation of recognition and legal action.

Only recognized organizations can carry the BulSU name, reserve campus facilities, collect approved dues, hold events, or sell reasonably priced org merchandise such as Friday org shirts.

## Starting or renewing a student organization

Every recognized organization must meet two standing requirements:

- **Active members:** Maintain at least **8 regular enrolled members** in good standing.
- **Faculty or staff adviser:** Secure a full-time BulSU faculty member or non-teaching employee as adviser, up to 3 advisers for a renewable 1-year term. Part-time faculty with at least 2 consecutive semesters qualify only if no regular employee is available.

### New organizations (minimum 15 founding members)

- Letter of intent and College Dean endorsement
- Recognition application form, Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Adviser profile, adviser invitation, and signed acceptance letter
- Officer and member lists with signatures, plus signed voluntary membership forms (lists alone suffice for 50 or more members)
- Annual activity proposal and budget proposal

### Annual renewal (opens around June to September)

- Renewal application form, updated Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Updated officer and membership lists, plus adviser profile
- Activity proposal and budget proposal for the incoming year
- Audited financial report with official receipts or invoices and photocopies of thermal receipts
- Accomplishment report with photos and narrative documentation, plus Certificate of Completion

### Review timeline, appeals, and awards

OSOA notifies applicants within **2 weeks**. If recognition is denied, you have **10 days** to appeal in writing to the Dean of OSAS, who decides within **10 working days**.

Recognized organizations receive their certificates during *Balangayan sa Unibersidad* and compete for annual honors: Outstanding Student Organization, Outstanding Student Leader, and Outstanding Adviser.

## Requirements for conducting activities

Every event requires the organization president's and adviser's signatures before the activity and a narrative report afterward. Sports and team-building events also require written safety guidelines.

### In-campus face-to-face events

- **Documents:** Activity letter, proposal form, budget form, venue letter, photocopy of adviser's ID, organizer list, and parental consent if held outside school hours.
- **Supervision and approval:** Requires a **1:50** faculty or adviser companion ratio. Approved up to the VPAA, or the Campus Administrator on external campuses.

### Off-campus face-to-face events

- **Documents:** All in-campus documents, plus Compliance Checklist, organizer and participant lists, invitation letters, CHED endorsement, OSOA certification, and parental consent.
- **Supervision and approval:** Requires a **1:20** faculty companion ratio. Routed through OSOA, OSD, the OSAS Dean, and the VPAA to the University President.

### Online activities

- **Documents:** Activity letter, proposal form, and organizer and participant list. Add a program flow, competition rules, or donation-drive action plan when applicable.
- **Approval:** Approved through OSOA.

## Room reservations, fundraising, and campus posters

- **Reserving campus rooms:** Submit requests **at least 1 week ahead** after checking availability with the facility head. Regular classes always take priority, and one organization cannot book a room on another group's behalf.
- **Fundraising:** Limited to **1 major fundraising activity per academic year**, approved by the University President. Off-campus solicitation also requires a DSWD permit. Submit a financial report to OSOA within **2 weeks** after the activity.
- **Campus posters and flyers:** File a signed letter of intent with the original poster at OSOA, plus College Dean approval for college buildings. Mount posters only on designated bulletin boards using scotch tape, masking tape, or push pins for **at most 2 weeks**, and remove them the day after expiration.
