To graduate with Latin Honors at Bulacan State University, you must hit the General Weighted Average (GWA) bracket, avoid any disqualifying single-subject grade, meet residency and unit rules, and pass NSTP. BulSU also holds the annual Gold Gear Awards (President's and Dean's Lists) for continuing students.

## Latin Honors cutoffs and grade floors

| Overall GWA | Degree courses | Non-degree courses | Lowest allowed grade in any subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.00 - 1.20 | Summa Cum Laude | With Highest Honors | No grade lower than 2.00 |
| 1.21 - 1.45 | Magna Cum Laude | With High Honors | No grade lower than 2.25 |
| 1.46 - 1.75 | Cum Laude | With Honors | No grade lower than 2.50 |

### Residency and unit rules

In addition to GWA and grade floors, you must meet both requirements:

- **Residency and credits:** Complete at least **75% of total academic units** required for your course and stay at least **2 years in residence** at BulSU.
- **15-unit minimum load:** Enroll in at least **15 prescribed credit units** every term, except when your curriculum schedules fewer than 15 units or needed subjects are not offered that term.

### How GWA is computed

1. Multiply each subject's numerical grade by its unit weight to get Grade Points.
2. Add all Grade Points and divide by your total academic units to get your Final GWA.

For example, five 3-unit subjects with grades of 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 1.25, and 1.50 total 17.25 grade points across 15.0 units, giving a GWA of 1.150.

**NSTP is excluded from the GWA calculation**, though you still must pass it. If your GWA lands right on a cutoff boundary, confirm rounding rules directly with the Office of the University Registrar.

You can use the [BulSU GWA Calculator](https://gwacalculatorbulsu.netlify.app/) to compute your GWA.

## Annual Gold Gear Awards (President's and Dean's Lists)

Continuing 2nd- to 4th-year students can apply for the annual Gold Gear Awards. Eligibility is based on your **last two consecutive semesters**: the 2nd semester of the prior academic year and the 1st semester of the current academic year.

Being on the Dean's List is optional and does not affect Latin Honors eligibility as long as you meet the graduation GWA and grade requirements.

- **President's List:** GPA of **1.00 to 1.20** with no grade lower than 2.0.
- **Dean's List:** GPA of **1.21 to 1.75** with no grade lower than 2.0. Some colleges such as COE list 1.25 to 1.75, so verify your college's exact call.
- **Additional categories:** Many colleges also accept applications for provincial, regional, national, and international competition winners and special awards in journalism, athletics, performing arts, and student leadership.

### How college Gold Gear applications work

Each college or campus runs its own application process:

- **Filing window:** Usually opens from December through early February.
- **Unit checks:** Colleges may enforce minimum unit loads, such as CIT's minimum 15-unit requirement.
- **Submission format:** Varies by college—either an online form, a scanned Certificate of Grades submitted in a brown envelope to the Dean's Office, or a Program Chair endorsement.

**Late entries are not accepted**, and falsifying records carries disciplinary penalties.

## Other graduation awards

Even if a single grade rules out Latin Honors, BulSU awards four other distinctions at graduation:

- **Outstanding Student Award:** One recipient per program per college or campus. Requires a GPA of at least 1.75, no grade below 2.0, a minimum of 15 units, competition wins, and active membership in recognized organizations.
- **Best in Specialization Award:** One recipient per specialization. Chosen by a faculty committee based on top major performance, no grade below 2.0 in major subjects, and competition wins.
- **College Leadership Award:** One recipient per college or campus. Requires at least 15 units in the last two semesters with zero dropped, INC, or failing grades, adviser nomination, a strong leadership record, and no disciplinary record.
- **University Leadership Award:** One recipient university-wide. Open to graduating leaders from Student Government, recognized organizations, cultural groups, or student publications via a leadership folio submitted to the Dean of Student Affairs and Services.
