Campus organizations at BulSU are accredited and monitored by the **Office of Student Organizations and Activities (OSOA)** alongside the **BulSU-OSO Executive Board**. Check the [BulSU OSOA Main Campus Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/osobulsu) each academic year for the official list of Recognized Student Organizations (RSOs), upload portals, and letter templates.

## Joining organizations

Membership in recognized BulSU organizations is **voluntary and open to all students**, including incoming freshmen. You are free to join more than one organization, subject to each group's constitution and voting rules.

Recognized groups span seven categories: college-based and campus-based academic societies, socio-civic, environmental, lifestyle, political, multi-faith (OSOA stays faith-neutral), and fraternities. BulSU caps university-wide recognition at **a maximum of 3 fraternities** at any time, and any fraternity violating university rules or the Anti-Hazing Law (RA 8049) faces automatic revocation of recognition and legal action.

Only recognized organizations may carry the BulSU name, reserve campus facilities, collect approved dues, hold events, or sell reasonably priced org merchandise (such as Friday org shirts).

## Starting or renewing a student organization

Every recognized organization must keep at least **8 regular enrolled members in good standing** and secure a full-time BulSU faculty or non-teaching employee as **adviser** (up to 3 advisers for a renewable 1-year term; part-time faculty with at least 2 consecutive semesters qualify only if no regular employee is available).

**For new organizations (minimum 15 founding members):**

- Letter of intent and College Dean endorsement
- Recognition application form, Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Adviser profile, adviser invitation, and signed acceptance letter
- Officer and member lists with signatures (signed voluntary membership forms; lists suffice for 50+ members)
- Annual activity proposal and budget proposal

**For annual renewal (opens around June to September):**

- Renewal application form, updated Constitution and By-Laws, and organizational chart
- Updated officer and membership lists plus adviser profile
- Activity proposal and budget proposal for the incoming year
- Financial report (audited with official receipts/invoices and photocopies of thermal receipts)
- Accomplishment report with photos and narrative documentation, plus Certificate of Completion

OSOA notifies applicants within **2 weeks**. If recognition is denied, you have **10 days** to appeal in writing to the **Dean of OSAS**, who decides within **10 working days**. Recognized organizations receive their certificates and compete for annual excellence honors (**Outstanding Student Organization**, **Leader**, and **Adviser**) during **Balangayan sa Unibersidad**.

## Requirements for conducting activities

Every event needs the organization president's and adviser's signatures plus narrative report submission afterward. Specific documents depend on your event format:

| Event type | Required documents | Supervision and approvals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-campus face-to-face | Activity letter, proposal form, budget form, venue letter, adviser ID photocopy, organizer list, parental consent (if outside school hours) | Faculty/adviser companion ratio of **1:50**; approved up to VPAA (or Campus Administrator on external campuses) |
| Off-campus face-to-face | All in-campus forms plus Compliance Checklist, organizer and participant lists, invitation letters, CHED endorsement, OSOA certification, and parental consent | Stricter faculty companion ratio of **1:20**; routed through OSOA, OSD, OSAS Dean, and VPAA to the **University President** |
| Online activities | Activity letter, proposal form, and organizer/participant list | Approved through OSOA; add program flow, competition rules, or donation-drive action plan when applicable |

Sports and team-building events also require written safety guidelines.

## Room reservations, fundraising, and campus posters

- **Reserving campus rooms:** Submit requests **at least 1 week ahead** after checking availability with the facility head. Regular classes always take priority, and one organization cannot book a room on another group's behalf.
- **Fundraising:** Limited to **1 major fundraising activity per academic year**, approved by the University President (plus a **DSWD permit** if soliciting off campus), with a financial report submitted to OSOA within **2 weeks**.
- **Campus posters and flyers:** File a signed letter of intent with the original poster at OSOA (plus College Dean approval for college buildings). Mount only on designated bulletin boards using scotch tape, masking tape, or push pins for **at most 2 weeks**, and remove them the day after expiration.
