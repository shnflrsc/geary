Failing 1 subject at Bulacan State University triggers a formal warning, while multiple failures or drops in a single semester can trigger prerequisite locks, reduced loads, academic probation, or disqualification. Confirm with your College Dean if your college enforces stricter retention cutoffs.

## Progressive academic delinquency tiers

Each semester, your College Dean and unit head review failing grades (5.00) and drops under 7 progressive tiers:

| Failures or drops in 1 term | Resulting standing | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 failing grade | Warning | You receive an official warning from your College Dean |
| 2 dropped or failed subjects | Prerequisite block | You cannot enroll in next-term requisite subjects; with Dean approval, you may take minor subjects ahead within your curriculum unit cap |
| 3 failed subjects, below 50% of load | Academic Probation | You enter probation next semester and your unit head reduces your academic load proportionally |
| On probation and 2 failures or drops | Dropped from course | You are removed from your current program and may apply to shift to another program with acceptance from the receiving Dean and approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA) |
| 50% to 75% failed subjects | Suggested shift | BulSU advises you to shift to another program, subject to the receiving Dean's acceptance and VPAA approval |
| More than 75% failed subjects | Disqualified | You are disqualified from continuing at BulSU |
| On probation and 50% or more failed subjects | Disqualified | You are disqualified from continuing at BulSU |

Dropped subjects count alongside failing marks under the 2-subject prerequisite block and the probation 2-subject removal rule.

Warning, probation, or shifting does not extend your maximum residency period:

| Program length | Maximum residency |
| --- | --- |
| 2-year courses | 4 years |
| 4-year courses | 6 years |
| 5-year courses | 7.5 years |

## Impact on graduation, honors, and scholarships

A past failing grade or irregular schedule does not stop you from graduating. You remain eligible as long as you pass every required curriculum subject, clear all INC and 4.00 marks, settle clearances, resolve any disciplinary holds, and finish at least 1 year of residency before graduation.

Failing grades affect awards and financial aid:

- Latin Honors require no grade lower than 2.00 for Summa Cum Laude, 2.25 for Magna Cum Laude, or 2.50 for Cum Laude across all subjects.
- The President's List and Dean's List (Gold Gear Awards) require no grade lower than 2.0 across the last 2 consecutive semesters.
- Student Assistantships and most scholarships require 0 failing or incomplete marks in the prior term.

## Recover if you fall behind

- Schedule an individual follow-up session with the Guidance and Counseling Services Center, which supports probationary students and students with low grades in rebuilding study habits.
- Petition for a special class if a failed subject is not offered next semester. Irregular students need at least **10 requesting students**, Dean approval, and filing within the college's semester petition window.

If you face dismissal from BulSU, the Office of the University Registrar still issues your transfer credentials once your clearance is complete.
