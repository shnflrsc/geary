## How to get

**Location**
- Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) (to log the lost ID)
- Office of the University Registrar (to apply and claim)
- Cashier's Office (for payment)

**Requirements**
- Active enrollment for the current term
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or another accepted ID)
- Original notarized Affidavit of Loss
- Official Receipt for PHP 150
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Report your missing ID to OSAS and the Office of the University Registrar so the loss is logged.
2. Have a notary public prepare and notarize an Affidavit of Loss stating how and when the ID went missing.
3. Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window to verify your enrollment and get a payment slip.
4. Pay PHP 150 at the Cashier's Office and keep the Official Receipt.
5. Submit your Affidavit of Loss, Official Receipt, and valid ID at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
6. Return on the scheduled release date with your claim stub and valid ID to pick up your duplicate ID.

## Fee and processing time

A duplicate ID costs PHP 150 and takes about 2 working days to print and release.

## Temporary gate entry

BulSU enforces a strict No ID, No Entry policy at every campus gate:

- **Lost ID:** Security accepts your notarized Affidavit of Loss as a temporary gate pass for 3 days only.
- **ID left at home:** Present your current Certificate of Registration (COR) to the gate guard for a signed remark on the back.

## If you find your original ID

Visit OSAS to cancel the loss report on your file.

## Penalties for ID tampering

Never alter ID details, swap photos, or lend your card to someone else. If security catches a tampered ID at the gate, the guard confiscates it on the spot and escorts you to your College Dean for disciplinary action.
