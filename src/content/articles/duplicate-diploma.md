## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Notarized Affidavit of Loss (or the damaged original diploma)
- Official Receipt for PHP 300
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Present your valid ID and notarized Affidavit of Loss (or damaged original diploma) at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
2. Pay PHP 300 at the Cashier's Office and keep the Official Receipt.
3. Submit your Official Receipt and Affidavit of Loss or damaged diploma at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
4. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub and valid ID, then sign the release logbook.

## Fee and processing time

A Duplicate Diploma costs PHP 300 and takes up to 8 working days to process.
