## Student load classifications

| Classification | Academic load and credit rules |
| --- | --- |
| Regular student | Carries the full academic load set for the semester in the curriculum |
| Irregular student | Enrolls for formal credit while carrying less than the full semester load |
| Working student | Holds a part-time job while studying and may enroll in at least 15 academic units per semester |
| Special student | Enrolls without earning academic credit for up to 9 units per semester and a maximum of 2 academic years, graded Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory |

Exemptions to the special student cap require a recommendation from the unit head and approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA).

### Missing subjects during online enrollment

Regular students can still finish enrollment in ALAB SMS if some subjects are not listed yet while schedules are still being posted. Those subjects stay part of your load and appear automatically once scheduled.

## Prerequisite enrollment blocks

Two rules stop you from advancing to dependent subjects:

- An INC grade in a prerequisite subject blocks enrollment in the next subject until you complete and clear it.
- Failing or dropping **2 subjects** in 1 term blocks enrollment in requisite subjects the next semester. With your Dean's approval, you may take minor subjects ahead as long as you stay within your curriculum unit limit.

## Petition for tutorial or special classes

When a required subject is not offered or conflicts with your schedule, BulSU offers 2 petition tracks.

### Tutorial classes for graduating seniors

Tutorial classes are strictly for graduating seniors who only need **1 remaining unoffered subject** to graduate that term. You need 3 approvals to qualify:

- Graduating-status certification from the Office of the University Registrar
- Recommendation from your College or Campus Dean
- Approval from the VPAA for Main Campus students, or the Vice President for Executive Operations for external campus students

### Special classes for irregular students

Special classes are for irregular students who need to repeat a subject or take one in advance when it is not offered or conflicts with their schedule. Opening a special class requires:

- At least **10 requesting students** for the same subject
- Approval from your College or Campus Dean
- Payment from the requesting students to cover the instructor's honorarium

### File a class petition

Colleges and external campuses announce short filing windows before or during enrollment, especially for graduating students. Prepare these documents for face-to-face petition packets:

- Signed petition letter listing the unoffered subject
- Signed commitment letter confirming payment for petitioned subjects
- Photocopy of a parent or guardian's valid ID, signed on the copy

Submitted requests route through 5 offices in order:

1. Faculty assignment
2. Dean endorsement
3. Chancellor's Office
4. Office of the University Registrar
5. Accounting

Check your college or campus Registrar page for exact local deadlines and forms.

## Maximum residency limits

Your maximum completion window depends on the length of your program:

| Program length | Maximum residency |
| --- | --- |
| 2-year course | 4 years |
| 4-year course | 6 years |
| 5-year course | 7.5 years |

Three rules govern your residency clock:

- Official Leave of Absence (LOA) semesters do not count toward your maximum residency.
- Working students may request a residency extension with Dean recommendation and VPAA approval.
- Exceeding the limit does not stop you from finishing your degree, but you **forfeit your government tuition subsidy** and must pay full tuition rates set by the Finance Office.
