Incoming freshmen apply to Bulacan State University through the University Admissions Office (UAO) online portal, then submit hard copies on their scheduled campus date. Transferees, shifters, and second-degree applicants follow a separate schedule and cannot apply through this track.

## Eligibility, deadlines, and disqualifications

You can apply as a freshman if you are a Senior High School (SHS) candidate for graduation, an SHS graduate from a public or DepEd-recognized private school, or a graduate of an accredited secondary school abroad.

**Do not use this track if you have college units.** Transferees and shifters who apply through Freshman Admission will be barred from all BulSU programs. Once you are admitted and enrolled, you can only request an honorable dismissal after the semester starts.

### ATBulSU 2027 schedule

Online applications for A.Y. 2027-2028 opened on August 25, 2026. Official pages list slightly different closing dates:

| Office or campus | Online application deadline | Hard-copy submission deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [BulSU Bustos Campus](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1670200784630839/) | **November 27, 2026** | **November 28, 2026** |

Treat the earlier main-office dates as your cutoff. If you are applying to an external campus—Bustos, Hagonoy, Meneses, Sarmiento, San Rafael, or Plaridel Extension—follow that campus's Facebook page for local submission updates.

BulSU has not yet announced the ATBulSU 2027 exam date, result release, or slot reservation schedule. For a rough timeline, the prior ATBulSU 2026 cycle held its exam on March 1, 2026, released results on April 17, 2026, and ran slot reservations and reconsideration in May 2026.

Freshman on-site submission has **no fee**. Confirm on the portal or with UAO if any testing fee applies to your cycle.

### Grounds for disqualification

Any of these errors voids your application:

- Misrepresenting information on the portal or any submitted form
- Violating application instructions
- Failing to submit hard copies on your scheduled date
- **Creating multiple applications**
- Falsifying the Declaration Form

BulSU also warns against fixers offering admission to applicants who failed the entrance exam. The university will not honor transactions made outside official exam and screening channels.

## Grade requirements

Eligibility depends on your Grade 11 General Weighted Average (GWA) and subject grades:

| Program type | Minimum Grade 11 GWA | Subject or track rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board and licensure programs | 87% | At least 85% in all subjects |
| Engineering, Architecture, Accountancy | 87% | At least 87% in Math and Science |
| Secondary Education (BSEd) | 87% | At least 87% in your major subject, such as Math for BSEd Math |
| Nursing, Psychology, Medical Technology | 87% | At least 87% in Science |
| Non-board programs | 85% | None beyond the 85% GWA |
| College of Industrial Technology (CIT) | No GWA cutoff | Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) track graduates with NC I or NC II preferred |
| College of Sports, Exercise, and Recreation (CSER) | No GWA cutoff | Sports track and provincial, regional, or national athletes preferred |

On September 25, 2026, BulSU added two board programs for A.Y. 2027-2028: BS Real Estate Management and BS Customs Administration. Check the [BulSU Admissions Office Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice) for their program sheet and exact campus availability.

## Documents to prepare

Upload scanned copies during your online application, then submit physical copies of the same documents on your appointment date.

### Mandatory documents

- Certified True Copy of your Grade 11 School Form 9 (SF9) with school seal
- Application Form (Declaration Form) with a 2" x 2" ID photo, emailed to you after completing the online application
- Certified True Copy of Proof of Residency from your barangay
- Photocopy of a recent electric, water, or internet bill
- Photocopy of a recent Income Tax Return (ITR), Certificate of Tax Exemption, or notarized Barangay Certification of low or no income

### Optional documents for Affirmative Action points

Submit these only if they apply to you. They count toward the 20-point Affirmative Action share of your score.

**Group A — submit at most one document:**

- Indigenous Cultural Community or Indigenous People (ICC/IP) membership or barangay certificate
- Pantawid Pamilya Pilipino Program (4Ps) membership or barangay certificate
- Students with Special Needs (SSN) or Persons with Disabilities (PWD) ID or membership certificate
- Alternative Learning System (ALS) Accreditation and Equivalency Assessment and Certification

**Group B — submit at most one document:**

- Solo Parent ID, if you are the child of a solo parent
- Child's Birth Certificate, if you are a solo parent
- Death certificate of one or both parents, if you are a single or double orphan living with relatives
- Certification from a Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)-recognized orphanage or shelter, if orphaned or abandoned
- First-generation college student proof, such as an MSWDO certification, an Affidavit of Non-Graduation from an immediate family member, or a barangay or school certification. Confirm with UAO which format it requires.

**Other optional documents — submit any that apply:**

- Barangay or local government unit (LGU) certification if you live in a Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Area (GIDA)
- Certification from your school head or community, civic, or religious organization head naming your title and award for exemplary artistic or athletic ability
- Certificate from the University Extension Office if you are a BulSU Adopted Community member, or your parent's latest BulSU Employee ID if they are a regular or non-regular employee with at least one year of service

## Application steps

1. Check the latest campus announcements on the [BulSU Admissions Office Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUAdmissionsOffice) or your external campus Facebook page before applying.
2. Open the [BulSU Online Application Portal](https://admissions.bulsu.edu.ph/) and fill out the freshman application form using an active email address with free storage.
3. Choose your 1st Choice Program and an allied 2nd Choice Program. If you do not qualify for your first choice, UAO evaluates you for your second choice automatically.
4. Upload scanned copies of all mandatory documents and any applicable optional documents on or before your chosen appointment date.
5. Download the Declaration Form sent to your email after online submission, print it, and attach a 2" x 2" ID photo.
6. Submit the hard copies matching your uploaded scans to the UAO or your chosen external campus on your appointment date. Watch official pages for announced Saturday catch-up dates if you miss a slot. For ATBulSU 2027, catch-up Saturdays ran through November 14, 2026.
7. Wait for your confirmation email containing your ATBulSU Test Permit with your permit number, exam date, time, room, building, and testing center, then print the permit. Check spam folders regularly.
8. Take the ATBulSU exam and attend any college interview, specialization exam, skill test, or talent test if your college shortlists you.
9. Watch the BulSU website and the Admissions Office Facebook page for the list of qualifiers, then complete slot reservation and enrollment on your assigned college schedule.

## How BulSU scores and ranks applicants

UAO ranks applicants on a 100-point scale combining exam performance, Grade 11 records, SHS strand alignment, and Affirmative Action points. Across all board courses leading to licensure examinations, applicants must score **80% or higher** on the admission examination.

### All colleges except Engineering

| Criterion | No interview | With interview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATBulSU test score | 50% | 50% |
| Grade 11 GWA (SF9) | 15% | 10% |
| SHS strand alignment | 15% | 10% |
| College interview | N/A | 10% |
| Affirmative Action | 20% | 20% |
| Total | 100% | 100% |

SHS strand alignment awards points based on how closely your strand matches your chosen degree:

| Strand match | No interview | With interview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directly related strand | 15 points | 10 points |
| Allied strand | 10 points | 7 points |
| Unrelated strand | 5 points | 5 points |

Check with UAO for exact strand-to-program mappings, since the strand appendix is not included in the public guidelines file.

### College of Engineering

Engineering applicants take both ATBulSU and a separate College of Engineering (COE) Exam under Philippine Technological Council (PTC-ACBET) alignment:

| Criterion | Requirement | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATBulSU test score | Admission test result | 40% |
| COE Exam score | Engineering exam | 20% |
| Grade 11 GWA | School Form 9 (SF9) | 10% |
| SHS strand taken | STEM: 10, GAS: 5, other strands: 2 | 10% |
| Affirmative Action | Personal and socio-economic criteria | 20% |
| Total | | 100% |

### Affirmative Action points

Affirmative Action accounts for up to 20 points:

| Category | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Residency | 3 points for Bulacan residents, 2 points for other Region 3 provinces |
| Group A (ICC/IP, 4Ps, SSN or PWD, or ALS — one category only) | 3 points |
| Group B (solo parent, orphan or abandoned, or first-generation student — one category only) | 3 points |
| GIDA residence | 3 points |
| Exemplary artistic or athletic ability | 3 points |
| BulSU Adopted Community member or child of a BulSU employee | 5 points |

## Exam day and slot reservation (ATBulSU 2026 reference)

UAO has not posted exam-day or reservation rules for ATBulSU 2027 yet. Use these ATBulSU 2026 rules as a reference until the 2027 advisories come out.

### Exam-day rules

What to wear and bring:

- Wear a white shirt.
- Bring only a transparent envelope holding your printed test permit, valid ID, two black ballpoint pens, a small bottle of alcohol, and a transparent water bottle.

What to leave at home:

- Bags, phones, electronics, jewelry, valuables, food, and headwear. Religious hijab is allowed.

Only applicants may enter campus. Proctors check pockets and require eyes and ears to stay visible.

### Qualifier slot reservation

Main Campus qualifiers followed three steps on their college's assigned date:

1. Bring a printed copy of your admission email to the UAO for verification against the Master List of Qualifiers and an official stamp. During the 2026 cycle, UAO operated temporarily at the BulSU E-Library 2nd Floor.
2. Complete the Medical and Dental Examination at University Health Services, held temporarily at Valencia Hall in 2026. Bring your emailed Medical and Dental Assessment Form, a chest X-ray taken within the last 6 months, and medical clearance or PWD ID if applicable.
3. Submit your enrollment packet to the University Registrar Office in a long envelope labeled with your pooling number, name, course, and campus. Include:
   - 2" x 2" ID photo
   - Certificate of Good Moral Character
   - Grade 12 SF9
   - Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Birth Certificate photocopy
   - Signed email confirmation
   - Signed Medical and Dental Clearance Slip

If you are placed under reconsideration, monitor your inbox and spam folder closely. Reconsideration emails go out in batches with strict submission dates, and missing your emailed schedule or the final incomplete-requirements call forfeits the slot.
