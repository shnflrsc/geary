## How to get

**Location**
- Office of the University Registrar (Main Campus)
- Cashier's Office (for payment and documentary stamp)

**Requirements**
- 1 original valid ID (National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID)
- Official Receipt for PHP 25 per copy
- 1 documentary stamp (for Graduate English Proficiency certifications)
- Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney and 1 photocopy each of your ID and your representative's ID (if filing or claiming through a representative)

**Step-by-step**
1. Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window to verify your record and receive a payment slip.
2. Pay PHP 25 per copy at the Cashier's Office, keep the Official Receipt, and buy a documentary stamp if requesting an English Proficiency certification.
3. Submit your Official Receipt and documentary stamp (if applicable) at the Registrar's window to receive your claim stub.
4. Return on the release date stamped on your claim stub with your stub and valid ID, then sign the release logbook.

## Covered certifications

- Enrollment
- Graduation
- Candidate for Graduation
- Grading System
- Second Copy Latin Honor
- Graduate GWA
- Graduate English Proficiency
- Special-Order Exemption

## Fee and processing time

Each certification costs PHP 25 per copy and takes up to 4 working days to process (typical turnaround for simple certifications is around 2 working days).
