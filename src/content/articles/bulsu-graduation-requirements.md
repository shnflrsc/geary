## Mandatory graduation requirements

Beyond passing every subject in your curriculum, every candidate for graduation must complete these four requirements:

- **Application for Graduation:** File at the Office of the University Registrar once you finish your program requirements. For timing reference, June 2026 graduates had a June 15, 2026 cutoff. Late filings receive the lowest priority for Transcript of Records (TOR) processing.
- **Residency minimum:** Complete at least **1 year of residence** at BulSU before graduation. Latin Honors require 2 years of residency.
- **University clearance:** Settle all financial and property accountabilities with the library, laboratories, student organizations, and Cashier before commencement using your campus Registrar's current Student Clearance Form.
- **Disciplinary clearance:** Resolve all pending disciplinary charges and serve any sanctions in full before graduation.

## Avoiding final-semester delays

- **Unresolved `INC` or `4.00` grades:** Any open `INC` or `4.00` blocks graduation clearance and converts to `5.00` if you miss the Registrar's semester completion deadline. BulSU does not offer a separate graduation-only INC extension, so follow the regular Registrar completion dates.
- **One unoffered subject left:** Graduating seniors blocked by a single unoffered course can petition for a **Tutorial Class**. Secure a graduating-student certification from the Office of the University Registrar, get your Dean's endorsement, and obtain approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs for Main Campus students or the Vice President for Executive Operations for external campus students.

## Documents for claiming your "Graduated" TOR

Your Registrar releases a TOR marked "Graduated" only after you finish all graduation requirements and file your Application for Graduation. Make sure these records are complete in your student file:

- School Form 10 (Form 137)
- Original School Form 9 (Grade 12 Report Card)
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Birth Certificate
- Honorable Dismissal and official TOR copy for BulSU, for transferees only

If your TOR cannot be printed because of record discrepancies, watch your email for resolution instructions from your campus Registrar.

## Commencement exercises and the Loyalty Award

- **Schedule and attire:** Candidates are expected to attend commencement exercises on dates set by the Academic Council and wear the prescribed academic costume for both baccalaureate and commencement ceremonies. Confirm your college's final commencement venue on [Bulacan State University's Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/bulsuofficial) before traveling.
- **Graduation fees:** Confirm current graduation fees directly with your college or Registrar.
- **Loyalty Award:** Given at graduation to students who started Grade 7 at BulSU Laboratory High School and completed their bachelor's degree continuously at BulSU.
