BulSU's academic load classifications, prerequisite blocks, petitioned classes, and maximum residency limits control how many units you can enroll in each term and how long your tuition subsidy lasts.

## Student load classifications

- **Regular student:** Carries the full academic load set for the semester in the curriculum.
- **Irregular student:** Enrolled for formal credit while carrying less than the full semester load.
- **Working student:** Holds a part-time job while studying and may enroll in a minimum of **15 academic units** per semester.
- **Special student:** Enrolls without earning academic credit (at most **9 units** per semester for up to **2 academic years**, graded Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory; exemptions need unit head recommendation and Vice President for Academic Affairs [VPAA] approval).

**Missing subjects during regular online enrollment:** Regular students can still enroll even when some subjects do not appear yet in ALAB SMS because schedules are still being posted. Those subjects remain part of your load and appear automatically once scheduled.

## How prerequisites block enrollment

Two rules prevent you from advancing to dependent subjects:

- **Incomplete (`INC`) prerequisite:** An `INC` grade in a prerequisite subject blocks enrollment in the next subject until you complete and clear it.
- **Two failures or drops in one term:** Dropping or failing two subjects blocks enrollment in requisite subjects the next semester. With your Dean's approval, you may take minor subjects ahead without exceeding your curriculum unit limit.

## Petitioning for tutorial or special classes

When a subject you need is not offered or conflicts with your schedule, BulSU provides two petition tracks:

### Tutorial classes (graduating seniors only)

- Open strictly to graduating seniors when a single remaining unoffered subject will let you graduate that term.
- Requires a graduating-status certification from the Office of the University Registrar, recommendation from your College or Campus Dean, and approval from the VPAA (Main Campus) or Vice President for Executive Operations (external campuses).

### Special classes (irregular students)

- Open to irregular students who need to repeat a subject or take a subject in advance when it is unoffered or has a schedule conflict.
- Requires at least **10 requesting students** for the same subject and approval from your College or Campus Dean. Requesting students shoulder the instructor's honorarium (petitioned subjects require payment).

### How petition filing works

Colleges and external campuses announce short petition filing windows before or during enrollment (especially for graduating students). Typical face-to-face petition packets require:

- Signed petition letter listing the unoffered subject
- Signed commitment letter (confirming payment for petitioned subjects)
- Photocopy of a parent or guardian's valid ID, signed on the copy

Requests route from student submission to faculty assignment, Dean endorsement, Chancellor's Office, Office of the University Registrar, and Accounting. Check your college or campus Registrar page for exact local deadlines and forms.

## Maximum residency limits

Your maximum completion window depends on program length:

| Program length | Maximum residency |
| --- | --- |
| 2-year course | 4 years |
| 4-year course | 6 years |
| 5-year course | 7.5 years |

Three rules govern your residency clock:

- **Approved Leave of Absence:** Official LOA semesters do not count toward your maximum residency.
- **Working student extension:** Working students may request a residency extension upon Dean recommendation and VPAA approval.
- **Exceeding the limit:** You may still finish your degree, though your government tuition subsidy is forfeited and you must pay full tuition rates set by the Finance Office.
