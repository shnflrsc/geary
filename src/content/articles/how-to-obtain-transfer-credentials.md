When you leave Bulacan State University to enroll in another college or university, your next school will ask for your Transfer Credentials, also called an Honorable Dismissal, from the Office of the University Registrar. Processing costs **PHP 25** and takes about **4 working days** after you submit complete requirements.

If you are a newly admitted freshman who has already enrolled in a BulSU program, you can only be granted an honorable dismissal once the semester officially starts.

## What to prepare

### Required for all applicants

- One original valid ID, such as a National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS, PhilHealth, TIN, Pag-IBIG, PRC, Company ID, or school ID
- Official Receipt for PHP 25 from the Cashier's Office

### Conditional requirements

- **If you have not graduated yet:** Accomplished Clearance Form, available at the Registrar window or on the BulSU website
- **If you have not submitted admission credentials yet:** Original Form 137 or SF-10 for freshmen, or original Transcript of Records (TOR) from your prior college for previous transferees, remarked `Copy for: Bulacan State University`
- **If filing or claiming through a representative:** Signed authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney, plus one photocopy of your ID and one photocopy of your representative's ID

## Step-by-step process

1. **Clear all accountabilities if you have not graduated.** Have your Clearance Form signed by your College Dean's Office, Library, Accounting Office, Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS), and the Office of the University Registrar. If you handled laboratory equipment or student organization property, settle those with your college first.
2. **Verify your record at the Registrar.** Present your valid ID at the Registrar's window so staff can check your student record and issue a payment slip.
3. **Pay the fee at the Cashier's Office.** Pay **PHP 25** and keep the Official Receipt.
4. **Submit your documents and get a claim stub.** Turn in your Official Receipt, signed Clearance Form if applicable, and any missing admission credentials at the designated Registrar window.
5. **Claim your Transfer Credentials.** Return on the release date shown on your claim stub, usually after 4 working days, with your stub and valid ID to pick up your Honorable Dismissal.

## Getting your official TOR sent to your new school

Your Honorable Dismissal lets your new university admit you and usually comes with a return slip or request form from your receiving school. Bring that original return slip back to the BulSU Registrar when requesting your TOR with the remark `Copy for: [Name of New School]`.

A non-graduated TOR costs **PHP 50 per page** and requires one documentary stamp. Processing takes about **6 working days** for records from 2008 to present, or up to **13 working days** for records from 2007 and earlier.

## What if you were dismissed or faced academic delinquency?

Even if you were dismissed or academically disqualified from continuing your program at BulSU, you still have the right to transfer out and receive your credentials:

- BulSU cannot block a dismissed or academically delinquent student from transferring to another school once all financial and property clearances are settled.
- The university can hold your credentials only for unpaid financial obligations or a pending disciplinary case awaiting final decision. Once resolved, standard release timelines apply, with an outer legal limit of 30 days from a complete request.
