Whether your issue involves unexplained grades, lack of academic consideration during typhoons, unfair treatment from staff or faculty, or harassment, filing through the right office gets you faster action than sending messages at random.

## Pick the right channel first

| Type of concern | Where to file first |
| --- | --- |
| Grade consultation or unannounced grade posting | Narrative report to your Class Mayor, escalated to your Local Student Council (plus active SG grievance forms like TAMBAD, SIWALAT, or college LSC forms) |
| Lack of academic consideration during flooding or weather disruptions | [BulSU Student Grievance Form](https://tinyurl.com/BulSUStudentGrievanceForm) via [BulSU SG Office of the Vice President](https://www.facebook.com/bulsusgovp) (`bulsusg.ovp@gmail.com`) |
| Unfair treatment, abuse of authority, or poor service | Director for Student Welfare (DSW) under Student Grievance Intervention (or Dean of OSAS if the complaint is against the DSW) |
| Bullying, abuse, hazing, or misconduct seeking penalties | College or campus Student Discipline Committee |
| Gender-based sexual harassment | [BulSU Gender and Development (GAD) Center](https://www.facebook.com/gadcbulsu) and university CODI channels |

## What to prepare before filing

Pull your documentation together before submitting any grievance so the receiving office can act right away:

- Written narrative stating your full name, student details, course or office involved, exact dates, and a clear factual account of what happened
- Supporting screenshots, emails, grade sheets, class messages, or documents
- Sworn witness statements or affidavits (if filing a formal disciplinary complaint)
- Active email address and mobile number for follow-ups

## Grade consultation and weather-related grievances

If an instructor refuses to explain how your grade was computed or posts final grades without consultation:

1. Write a clear narrative report with your personal details, course information, and what happened.
2. Submit it first to your **Class Mayor**.
3. If the issue stays unresolved, escalate it to your **Local Student Council** for coordination with your college administration or OSAS, and submit through the current semester's Student Government grade-transparency form (posted each term under campaigns like **TAMBAD** by SG Academic Affairs, **SIWALAT** by the Office of the Student Regent, or college-specific LSC drives like CCJE's **PAGLANTAD**).

For academic consideration complaints during typhoons, widespread flooding, or power outages, file directly through the **BulSU Student Grievance Desk** managed by the SG Office of the Vice President (`bulsusg.ovp@gmail.com`).

## General unfair treatment (Student Grievance Intervention)

For broader issues involving unfair treatment, abuse of authority, or poor instruction or service from any classmate, instructor, staff member, or administrator, BulSU provides **Student Grievance Intervention**.

**Direct-victim rule:** You must be the direct victim of the unfair treatment. Friends, classmates, or parents cannot file a formal grievance on your behalf.

1. **Try informal resolution first (encouraged, not required):** Speak directly with the person involved or send them a written letter describing the concern. They are expected to respond promptly through a dialogue or written reply.
2. **File formally with the DSW or Student Government:** Submit your written complaint to the **Director for Student Welfare (DSW)** (or directly to the **Dean of Student Affairs and Services / OSAS** if your complaint is against the DSW).
3. **Screening and formal endorsement:** The DSW checks validity, mediates when possible, and sends formal representations to the college, department, or office involved without acting as judge.
4. **Follow-ups and automatic escalation:** The DSW follows up until resolved. If an office ignores or stalls the endorsement, the DSW elevates the complaint to the next higher office along with a complaint of inaction, all the way up to the **University President**.

## Disciplinary complaints, harassment, and peaceful assemblies

- **Seeking formal disciplinary penalties:** File sworn written statements, witness affidavits, and documentary evidence directly with your college or campus **Student Discipline Committee** (see [Understanding University Disciplinary Procedures](/articles/understanding-university-disciplinary-procedures)).
- **Gender-based sexual harassment:** Covered under the Anti-Sexual Harassment Act (RA 7877) and the Safe Spaces Act (RA 11313). Contact the [BulSU Gender and Development (GAD) Center](https://www.facebook.com/gadcbulsu), which runs Legal Aid Clinics and shares official reporting flowcharts and guidance alongside university CODI procedures.
- **Group grievances and peaceful assemblies:** Submit a formal position paper for dialogue first. To hold a peaceful on-campus assembly, recognized sponsors must file a written request with the **University President at least 2 school days before** the event or announcement, listing venue, duration, estimated attendees, course/year/section, invited speakers, and signatures of the organization president and adviser, while keeping gates, roads, and classes clear.

**Filing an accident or medical insurance claim instead?** That is not a grievance. For animal or insect bites, personal accidents, or road accidents, visit the **Office of Student Welfare** within **30 days** of the incident to get the Enhanced Medical Insurance claim checklist (full documents are due within **90 days**). See [BulSU Medical and Health Services Guide](/articles/bulsu-medical-and-health-services-guide) for health support details.
