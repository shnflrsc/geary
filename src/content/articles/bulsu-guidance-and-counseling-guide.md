College brings academic, personal, and family pressure, and BulSU's **Guidance and Counseling Services Center (GCSC)** provides free, confidential support so you do not have to work through it alone.

**Where to find GCSC (Main Campus):** **RH 104-105, Roxas Hall**. You can call `(044) 919-7800 local 1071 or 1072`, email `bulsuguidancecenter@bulsu.edu.ph`, message [BulSU GCSC Main Campus on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUGCSCMain), or book a session directly through the [GCSC Counseling Appointment Form](https://forms.gle/QZRMGU8yjAyRNnmL8).

## Six core services at GCSC

1. **Counseling Service:** Confidential one-on-one or group counseling when stress, anxiety, academic struggles, relationship concerns, or major decisions feel overwhelming. You may book voluntarily, walk in, or be referred by a faculty member or office.
2. **Testing Service:** Standardized psychological and readiness assessments with counselor interpretation, including the **Mental Ability Test (MAT)** for 1st-year students, **College Adjustment Scale (CAS)** for 2nd-year students, **OMNI Personality Inventory** for 3rd-year students, the **Psycho-Emotional Preparedness Survey for Internship**, and scholarship or graduate admission tests.
3. **Individual Inventory Service:** Builds your confidential Student Cumulative Record starting from freshman or transferee entry to help counselors match you with the right support.
4. **Information Service:** Seminars, webinars, Peer Facilitators training, and guides on campus adjustment, mental health, and career direction.
5. **Follow-up Service:** Monitors student progress after counseling, conducts graduating Exit Interviews, and reaches out to students facing low grades or academic probation to help rebuild study habits and coping skills.
6. **Referral Service:** Connects students who need specialized outside medical, psychological, financial, or family support to qualified professionals and partner agencies.

## Mental health crisis hotlines

If you or a friend need urgent emotional support outside campus office hours, reach out to these free Philippine crisis lines shared by GCSC:

- **DOH-NCMH Crisis Hotline:** `1553` (landline) or `0919-057-1553`
- **Hopeline Philippines:** `0917-558-4673`
- **In Touch Crisis Line:** `(02) 8893-7603`, `0919-056-0709`, or `0917-800-1123`

## Support for students with special needs and PWDs

Under Republic Act 7277, BulSU maintains a **Committee on Students with Special Needs and Persons with Disabilities (SWSN / PWDs)** bringing together Student Services, Health Services, Guidance and Counseling, and campus facilities staff. The committee:

- Meets with students, professors, parents or guardians, and personal assistants to arrange tailored **academic accommodations**
- Coordinates counseling, testing referrals, and life-skills workshops
- Works with campus safety staff to check building accessibility and prioritize PWD assistance during emergency evacuations

For career readiness and internship counseling near graduation, check [How Internships and Job Placement Work at BulSU](/articles/how-internships-work-at-bulsu).
