Graduating with Latin Honors at Bulacan State University requires hitting the General Weighted Average (GWA) bracket, avoiding any disqualifying single-subject grade, meeting university residency and unit rules, and passing NSTP. BulSU also holds the annual **Gold Gear Awards** (President's and Dean's Lists) for continuing students.

## Latin Honors cutoffs and grade floors

| Overall GWA | Degree courses | Non-degree courses | Lowest allowed grade in any subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.00 - 1.20 | Summa Cum Laude | With Highest Honors | No grade lower than 2.00 |
| 1.21 - 1.45 | Magna Cum Laude | With High Honors | No grade lower than 2.25 |
| 1.46 - 1.75 | Cum Laude | With Honors | No grade lower than 2.50 |

### Residency and unit rules

In addition to GWA and grade floors, candidates must satisfy:

- **Residency and credits:** Complete at least **75% of total academic units** required for the course and stay at least **2 years in residence** at BulSU.
- **15-unit minimum load:** Enroll at least **15 prescribed credit units** every term, except when your curriculum schedules fewer than 15 units or needed subjects are not offered that term.

### How GWA is computed

1. Multiply each subject's numerical grade by its unit weight (`Grade x Units = Grade Points`).
2. Add all Grade Points and divide by total academic units (`Total Grade Points / Total Units = Final GWA`).

For example, five 3-unit subjects with grades `1.00`, `1.00`, `1.00`, `1.25`, and `1.50` total `17.25` grade points across `15.0` units, giving a GWA of `1.150`. **NSTP is excluded from the GWA calculation**, though you still must pass it. Confirm rounding rules directly with the Office of the University Registrar if your GWA lands right on a boundary.

You can use the [BulSU GWA Calculator](https://gwacalculatorbulsu.netlify.app/) to compute your GWA.

## Annual Gold Gear Awards (President's and Dean's Lists)

Continuing students (2nd to 4th year) can apply for the annual Gold Gear Awards based on their **last two consecutive semesters** (2nd semester of the prior academic year and 1st semester of the current academic year).

Being on the Dean's List is optional and does not affect Latin Honors eligibility as long as you meet the graduation GWA and grade requirements.

- **President's List:** GPA of **1.00 to 1.20** with no grade lower than `2.0`
- **Dean's List:** GPA of **1.21 to 1.75** with no grade lower than `2.0` (some colleges such as COE list `1.25 to 1.75`, so verify your college's exact call)
- **Additional categories:** Many colleges also accept applications for provincial, regional, national, and international competition winners and special awards (such as journalism, athletics, performing arts, and student leadership).

### How college Gold Gear applications work

Each college or campus sets its own filing window (usually December through early February), unit check (such as CIT's minimum 15-unit requirement), and submission format (online form, scanned Certificate of Grades in a brown envelope to the Dean's Office, or Program Chair endorsement). Late entries are not accepted, and falsifying records carries disciplinary penalties.

## Other graduation awards

Even if a single grade rules out Latin Honors, BulSU awards four other distinctions at graduation:

- **Outstanding Student Award** (1 per program per college or campus): GPA of at least `1.75`, no grade below `2.0`, minimum 15 units, competition wins, and active membership in recognized orgs.
- **Best in Specialization Award** (1 per specialization): top major performance with no grade below `2.0` in major subjects and competition wins, chosen by a faculty committee.
- **College Leadership Award** (1 per college or campus): at least 15 units in the last two semesters with zero dropped, `INC`, or failing grades, adviser nomination, strong leadership record, and no disciplinary record.
- **University Leadership Award** (1 university-wide): open to graduating leaders from Student Government, recognized orgs, cultural groups, or student publications via a leadership folio submitted to the Dean of Student Affairs and Services.
