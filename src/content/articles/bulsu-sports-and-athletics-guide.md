The Sports Development Office (SDO) and the Office of Culture and Arts (OCA) coordinate varsity athletics and performing arts at BulSU.

## Join BulSU varsity teams

Follow [BulSU Sports Development Office on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bulsusdo) and individual Gold Gears team pages for open tryout schedules. Tryouts usually take place at the BulSU Activity Center, the Bulacan Sports Complex, or partner sports courts.

Students enter varsity rosters through 2 paths:

- Incoming freshmen or transferees qualify through external athlete recruitment with at least a bronze medal in Palarong Pambansa or an equivalent national meet, a silver medal in a regional meet, or a gold medal in a provincial meet. Division and district placements do not qualify.
- Enrolled students join open campus tryouts or get scouted during University Intramurals. Individual teams may set year-level rules, competition background requirements such as BULPRISA or City Meet experience, or personal training gear requirements.

Coaches evaluate athlete candidates using 5 weighted criteria:

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Attitude | 30% |
| Level of competition | 20% |
| Sports skills | 20% |
| Academic grades | 20% |
| Age | 10% |

### Competitions, scholarships, and athlete rules

Varsity athletes train year-round and represent BulSU in meets such as the SCUAA III Regional Olympics and UCLAA. BulSU topped the 2026 SCUAA III official medal tally with 121 golds and 262 total medals.

- Qualified athletes receive **50% to 100% tuition scholarships**. Full scholars cannot fail more than **10%** of their enrolled units.
- Athletes may apply to stay in the Athletes' Dormitory with approval from their coach and the Sports Development Office.
- Joining non-university community games requires a medical permit, parental consent, and Sports Development clearance.

## Join cultural and performing arts groups

The [BulSU Office of Culture and Arts](https://www.facebook.com/BulSUOCA) recruits student performers through annual auditions and campus showcases such as SALIKSIK. Categories include vocal competitions, instrumental solo, literary arts (essay, storytelling, monologue, and oratory), and visual arts (painting, rendering, and photography).

Recognized university performing groups include Hyper-Dynamics, the university Band, Lahing Kayumanggi Dance Troupe, Saring Himig Chorale, and Euphonistas, alongside college-level cultural clubs.

- Qualified members may receive **50% to 100% tuition discounts** on their adviser's recommendation. Confirm current monthly allowance terms with OSFAS or OCA.
- Full cultural scholars receive a warning at **1 failing mark** and must pass all subjects the next semester. Reaching **3 failing marks** revokes the grant.
- Cultural performers are exempted from BulSU's natural hair-color rule when required by an official competition or stage show.

## Request off-campus competition clearances

Before leaving campus for official contests, seminars, or guestings, complete these approvals in order:

1. File the Compliance Checklist with the Office of Student Development.
2. Secure recommending approval from the OSAS Dean.
3. Obtain the University President's signature.

Off-campus group travel must also follow the **1:20 faculty companion ratio**. Official university representation counts as an excused class absence, up to the 30% semester cap.

## Competition cash incentives

BulSU grants baseline cash incentives to regional and national winners, plus 50% of the prize amount for coaches. Confirm current payout rates with SDO or OCA, as university rates may be updated.

| Competition category | Level | 1st place | 2nd place | 3rd place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Academic, Sports, and Cultural | Regional | PHP 1,000 | PHP 700 | PHP 500 |
| Academic, Sports, and Cultural | National | PHP 1,500 | PHP 1,200 | PHP 800 |
| Pageants and Beauty Contests | Regional | PHP 5,000 | PHP 3,000 | PHP 1,000 |
| Pageants and Beauty Contests | National | PHP 10,000 | PHP 8,000 | PHP 5,000 |

Top student athletes, artists, and competition winners may also qualify for annual Gold Gear Awards covered in [Can I Graduate With Honors?](/articles/can-i-graduate-with-honors).
