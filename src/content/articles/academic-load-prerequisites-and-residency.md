## Student load classifications

- **Regular student:** Carries the full academic load set for the semester in the curriculum.
- **Irregular student:** Enrolls for formal credit while carrying less than the full semester load.
- **Working student:** Holds a part-time job while studying and may enroll in at least **15 academic units** per semester.
- **Special student:** Enrolls without earning academic credit and receives a grade of Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory. Special students may take up to **9 units** per semester for a maximum of **2 academic years**. Any exemption requires a recommendation from the unit head and approval from the Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA).

**Missing subjects during online enrollment:** Regular students can still complete enrollment in ALAB SMS even if some subjects are not listed yet because schedules are still being posted. Those subjects stay part of your load and appear automatically once scheduled.

## How prerequisites block enrollment

Two rules stop you from advancing to dependent subjects:

- **Incomplete (`INC`) prerequisite:** An `INC` grade in a prerequisite subject blocks enrollment in the next subject until you complete and clear it.
- **Two failures or drops in one term:** Dropping or failing two subjects blocks enrollment in requisite subjects the next semester. With your Dean's approval, you may take minor subjects ahead as long as you stay within your curriculum unit limit.

## Petitioning for tutorial or special classes

When a subject you need is not offered or conflicts with your schedule, BulSU offers two petition tracks.

### Tutorial classes for graduating seniors

Tutorial classes are strictly for graduating seniors who only need one remaining unoffered subject to graduate that term. To qualify, you need:

- A graduating-status certification from the Office of the University Registrar
- A recommendation from your College or Campus Dean
- Approval from the VPAA for Main Campus students, or the Vice President for Executive Operations for external campus students

### Special classes for irregular students

Special classes are for irregular students who need to repeat a subject or take one in advance when it is not offered or conflicts with their schedule. Opening a special class requires:

- At least **10 requesting students** for the same subject
- Approval from your College or Campus Dean
- Payment from the requesting students to cover the instructor's honorarium

### How petition filing works

Colleges and external campuses announce short filing windows before or during enrollment, especially for graduating students. Face-to-face petition packets typically require:

- Signed petition letter listing the unoffered subject
- Signed commitment letter confirming payment for petitioned subjects
- Photocopy of a parent or guardian's valid ID, signed on the copy

Once submitted, requests route through these offices in order:

1. Faculty assignment
2. Dean endorsement
3. Chancellor's Office
4. Office of the University Registrar
5. Accounting

Check your college or campus Registrar page for exact local deadlines and forms.

## Maximum residency limits

Your maximum completion window depends on the length of your program:

| Program length | Maximum residency |
| --- | --- |
| 2-year course | 4 years |
| 4-year course | 6 years |
| 5-year course | 7.5 years |

Three rules govern your residency clock:

- **Approved Leave of Absence:** Official Leave of Absence (LOA) semesters do not count toward your maximum residency.
- **Working student extension:** Working students may request a residency extension with Dean recommendation and VPAA approval.
- **Exceeding the limit:** You can still finish your degree, but you forfeit your government tuition subsidy and must pay full tuition rates set by the Finance Office.
